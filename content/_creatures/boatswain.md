      <h3 id="actions">Actions</h3>

<p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The captain makes three melee attacks: two with its scimitar and one with its dagger. Or the captain makes two ranged attacks with its daggers.</p>

<p><strong><em>Scimitar.</em></strong> Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage.</p>

<p><strong><em>Dagger.</em></strong> Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.</p>

<h3 id="reactions">Reactions</h3>

<p><strong><em>Parry.</em></strong> The captain adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the captain must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.</p>
