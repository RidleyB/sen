<h3 id="actions">Actions</h3>

<p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The blighted growth makes two Vine Lash attacks. If both hit the same Medium or smaller target, that target is grappled (escape DC 11).</p>

<p><strong><em>Vine Lash.</em></strong> Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d8 + 2) bludgeoning damage.</p>

<p><strong><em>Constrict.</em></strong> A creature grappled by the blighted growth takes 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage at the start of each of its turns. The blighted growth can only have one creature grappled at a time.</p>
