      <p><strong><em>Amorphous.</em></strong> The ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.</p>

<p><strong><em>Corrode Metal.</em></strong> Any nonmagical weapon made of metal that hits the ooze corrodes. After dealing damage, the weapon takes a permanent and cumulative -1 penalty to damage rolls. If its penalty drops to -5, the weapon is destroyed. Nonmagical ammunition made of metal that hits the ooze is destroyed after dealing damage.</p>

<p>The ooze can eat through 2-inch-thick, nonmagical metal in 1 round.</p>

<p><strong><em>False Appearance.</em></strong> While the ooze remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from an oily pool or wet rock.</p>

<h3 id="actions">Actions</h3>

<p><strong><em>Pseudopod.</em></strong> Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) acid damage, and if the target is wearing nonmagical metal armor, its armor is partly corroded and takes a permanent and cumulative -1 penalty to the AC it offers. The armor is destroyed if the penalty reduces its AC to 10.</p>
