      <p><strong><em>Rampage.</em></strong> When the beastman reduces a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack on its turn, it can take a bonus action to move up to half its speed and make a bite attack.</p>

<h3 id="actions">Actions</h3>

<p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The beastman makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its club, or two with its claw.</p>

<p><strong><em>Bite.</em></strong> Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d4+2) piercing damage.</p>

<p><strong><em>Claw.</em></strong> Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d4+2) slashing damage.</p>

<h3 id="reactions">Reactions</h3>

<p><strong><em>Ravenous Strike.</em></strong> In response to a creature being reduced to 0 hit points within 30 feet of the beastman, the beastman makes a melee attack.</p>
