      <p><strong><em>Rampage.</em></strong> When the beastman reduces a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack on its turn, the beastman can take a bonus action to move up to half its speed and make a bite attack.</p>

<h3 id="actions">Actions</h3>

<p><strong><em>Multiattack.</em></strong> The beastman makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its shortsword.</p>

<p><strong><em>Bite.</em></strong> Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d4+2) piercing damage.</p>

<p><strong><em>Shortsword.</em></strong> Melee Weapon Attack: +4 to hit, reach 5 ft, one target. Hit: 5 (1d6+2) piercing damage.</p>

<p><strong><em>Sudden Rush.</em></strong> Until the end of the turn, the beastman’s speed increases by 60 feet and it doesn’t provoke opportunity attacks.</p>
