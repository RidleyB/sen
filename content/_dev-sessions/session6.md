## Session 6 – *Ash and Iron*

**Goal:** Confront the Blackfish Runners at the warehouse, resolve the lockbox situation, end on the reveal that Blacktide has been seized.

**Target Runtime:** ~3 hours

* Departure & Road: 30–40 min
* Warehouse Camp & Confrontation: 90–110 min
* Fallout & Return: 30–40 min
* Cliffhanger: 10 min

## Leaving Blacktide

Before departure, present the choice plainly:

* **Sleep in Blacktide** (common room, ship, alley, etc.)
* **Push on immediately**
* **Travel partway and camp outside town**

Make clear the warehouse is several hours inland along an old, barely maintained horse-cart road cutting through dry Karthold scrubland.

### Random Encounter Roll (1x when they leave town)

1. **Dust Devil Collapse**
   Sudden spiraling winds tear across the road. Visibility drops.

   * DC checks to avoid minor damage or lost gear.
   * Tracks on the road become obscured (harder to track anyone later).

2. **Broken Caravan Axle**
   A merchant wagon sits stranded.

   * The merchant fears Beastmen after dark.
   * If helped, they share rumor: “Harrivale’s been asking questions about Blacktide.”

3. **Rockslide Echo**
   Distant rumbling from ridge lines.

   * Harmless, but reveals how exposed the road is.
   * A character notices old campfire remains nearby (someone has used this road recently).

4. **Scavenger Birds**
   Carrion birds circle something off-road: a dead Blackfish sailor, throat cut.

   * Suggests internal betrayal or silencing.
   * No insignia remains.

## If They Camp Outside Blacktide

Roll 1x if they sleep outside town.

Remember: Dimlight keeps Beastmen away unless the party extinguishes firelight.

1. **Beastmen Eyes in the Bramble**
   Only if they reduce fire or split watch.
   Shapes shift at the edge of shadow. They test but withdraw if met with flame.

2. **Brambled Ground Disturbance**
   Something heavy moved through the scrub recently (likely Viscera’s crew hauling supplies).

3. **Harrivale Scout Pair**
   Two mounted scouts pass at distance during Dimlight.
   If spotted, they are mapping roads and asking about “smuggling disruptions.”

4. **Dry Wind Howl**
   No combat — but unsettling auditory illusion of whispers in the scrub.

## The Warehouse Camp

This is the session’s core set-piece. The tension comes from fear, firelight, and time pressure.

### Location Tone

The ore warehouse sits in a shallow basin of cracked stone and bramble scrub. Rocky ridgelines create natural shadow pockets beyond the firelight.

The smugglers have turned the area into a **bright island in a hostile wasteland**:

* 6 tall oil lantern poles hammered into the dirt
* 2 braziers near the warehouse doors
* 1 central bonfire
* Lanterns hung along the warehouse exterior beams
* Light spills maybe 40–60 ft outward before fading into scrub darkness

Beyond that radius: shifting bramble, jagged rock, wind.

## Camp Composition

Increase presence for tension:

**10–14 Blackfish Runners total**

* 6 alert and armed (rotating perimeter)
* 3 nursing severe burns (hands wrapped, useless in combat)
* 2 arguing over whether to flee before Viscera returns
* 1 nervous lookout posted on warehouse roof with spyglass

Morale: Shaken.

They are not unified. They are afraid of:

* The lockbox
* Viscera
* Beastmen
* Harrivale (rumors spreading)

### Evidence of Failed Attempts

Near the central table:

* A warped crowbar fused at the tip
* Charred gloves stuck to wood
* A shattered chisel head
* Blackened scorch marks spiderwebbing outward from the box
* One sailor missing two fingertips

The box hums faintly when touched.

Anyone observing carefully notices:

* The burns look arcane, not flame-based.
* The reaction happens instantly on forced tampering.

Attempts have stopped. No one wants to touch it now.

### Smuggler Dialogue Snippets

Use to show fracture and fear:

> “He said he’d bleed us if it wasn’t open.”
> “Let him try. I’m not losing another hand.”
> “Maybe it ain’t meant to be opened.”
> “Shut up. He’ll hear you.”
> “What if Harrivale already knows?”

They’re primed to crack under pressure.

### Tactical Layout

### Exterior

* Camp arranged in a loose horseshoe around warehouse doors.
* Lockbox on reinforced crate near central fire.
* Most weapons stacked nearby but not all drawn.
* Lookout above has partial visibility of road.

### Interior

Warehouse mostly empty:

* Rotting ore bins
* Chain hoists
* Collapsed beams creating dark alcoves
* Shadowed upper rafters

If lights drop, interior becomes extremely dangerous.

## Player Option 1 – Douse the Lights

This is the most dynamic route.

If the party sabotages lanterns or braziers:

**Phase 1: Panic**

* Shouting
* Smugglers scrambling to relight
* Lookout screaming about movement in brambles

**Phase 2: Shadow Movement**
Beastmen test perimeter. They do not charge immediately.

They wait until:

* Light falls below half strength
* Smugglers scatter
* Obvious weakness shows

**Phase 3: Assault**
4–8 Beastmen surge from shadowed flanks.

They target:

* Isolated smugglers
* Anyone far from fire
* Injured first

They avoid the brightest flame zones unless numbers favor them.

This can create:

* Three-sided chaos
* Opportunity to steal the box
* Opportunity to vanish in confusion

If party keeps firelight up near themselves, Beastmen largely ignore them.

## Player Option 2 – Direct Assault

Smugglers are numerous but brittle.

Combat traits:

* Two attempt to flee early.
* Burned sailors do not fight effectively.
* If 4+ smugglers drop quickly, morale breaks.

If half fall, remaining may:

* Throw down weapons
* Attempt bargain
* Reveal Viscera left at dawn and will return soon

Keep combat brisk — they are not elite.

## Player Option 3 – Intimidation / Deception

Strong leverage angles:

* “Viscera is dead.”
* “Harrivale is coming.”
* “Captain Veylan sent us.”
* “The box kills whoever opens it.”

Because morale is low, a convincing display (especially magical) may splinter them.

If successful:

* 4–6 desert immediately
* 2 demand protection in exchange for info
* 1 may secretly run to warn Viscera (future hook)

## Player Option 4 – Social Manipulation

If the party offers:

* Protection
* Split of future loot
* Safe escort away

The smugglers might surrender the box willingly.

One burned sailor may confess:

> “Viscera said it was worth more than all Blacktide. Said we’d never bow again.”

This reinforces future villain arc.

## Environmental Escalation Lever

If the party lingers too long (over 45–60 min in-scene time):

Wind picks up.

Lantern flames flicker.

Beastmen numbers increase in outer darkness.

This pressures resolution.

## Important: Preserve Viscera

Evidence Viscera was recently here:

* Ledger with coded entries
* Distinct bootprints (pointed heel, clawed impression)
* Blood-stained handwraps discarded
* Orders written: “Open it before I return.”

No sign of where they went.

Smugglers believe Viscera will return by next day.

## Lockbox Handling During Scene

If players attempt to open:

* Immediate arcane backlash (scaled)
* Visible surge of sigils
* Burns that bypass mundane protection

If they experiment intelligently (arcane study), you can hint:

* It is sealed intentionally, not merely trapped.
* There is a condition for safe opening.

But do not resolve it here unless you want to.

## Ending the Warehouse Scene

Possible End States:

1. Party escapes with lockbox during Beastmen chaos.
2. Party defeats smugglers and claims it cleanly.
3. Party manipulates smugglers into dispersing.
4. Party withdraws after observing instability.

Whatever the outcome:
The firelight eventually shrinks.
The wilderness reclaims the basin.
