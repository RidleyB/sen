# Personalized Imar Whispers

## Creed — The Lost Sister

**Core Temptation:** Imar claims it can lead Creed to his sister.

### First Contact

> “Creed…”
>
> “I know where she is.”
>
> “She called for you in the dark.”
>
> “I heard her.”

### Deeper Contact

> “You have walked so far with empty hands.”
>
> “You have bled for strangers.”
>
> “You have chased shadows, rumors, graves.”
>
> “But I have touched the places beneath the world.”
>
> “I know where she went.”

### Failed Save / Stronger Intrusion

> “She is not lost to me.”
>
> “Nothing is lost beneath the earth.”
>
> “Give me your hand, and I will show you the path.”
>
> “You do not have to keep failing her.”

### Combat Whisper

> “They will slow you down.”
>
> “They will tell you to turn away.”
>
> “But they do not love her like you do.”

### Vision

Creed sees a figure ahead in the dark: familiar posture, familiar hair, vanishing around a bend in the cavern. He hears his sister’s voice whisper:

> “Why did you stop looking?”

### The Lie Beneath It

Imar does not necessarily know where Creed’s sister is. It knows what Creed would trade to believe that it does.

## Victor — The Stolen Family

**Core Temptation:** Imar offers Victor power, control, and freedom from loss.

### First Contact

> “They took everything from you.”
>
> “Disease.”
>
> “Time.”
>
> “Weakness.”
>
> “I can make you strong enough that nothing ever takes from you again.”

### Deeper Contact

> “You begged the world for mercy.”
>
> “It gave you graves.”
>
> “You sought power and found hunger.”
>
> “You tried to save them, and the world punished you for loving too fiercely.”
>
> “I would not punish you.”

### Failed Save / Stronger Intrusion

> “No more pleading.”
>
> “No more helpless hands.”
>
> “No more doors closing while the people you love rot behind them.”
>
> “Take what the world denied you.”
>
> “Defy it all.”

### Combat Whisper

> “Look how fragile they are.”
>
> “Bone. Blood. Breath.”
>
> “Everything you care for can be taken unless you become something no one dares touch.”

### Vision

Victor sees his wife and son as they were before sickness hollowed them. They are not undead. Not monstrous. They are warm, whole, smiling.

Then the vision flickers.

They are behind glass.

Drowning.

Waiting for him to become powerful enough to break it.

### The Lie Beneath It

Imar does not offer healing. It offers possession disguised as protection.

---

## Sonny — The Brighter World

**Core Temptation:** Imar promises Sonny a world free of suffering.

### First Contact

> “You see it, don’t you?”
>
> “The rot beneath every smile.”
>
> “The cruelty behind every law.”
>
> “The suffering around every corner.”
>
> “I can show you something brighter.”

### Deeper Contact

> “You call it cynicism because that is easier than admitting you are right.”
>
> “The world is cruel.”
>
> “The innocent suffer.”
>
> “The powerful feast.”
>
> “The kind are buried.”
>
> “But it does not have to remain this way.”

### Failed Save / Stronger Intrusion

> “Imagine a world without hunger.”
>
> “Without loneliness.”
>
> “Without children learning fear before language.”
>
> “Without good people dying in the mud while monsters wear crowns.”
>
> “I can make it gentle.”
>
> “I can make it clean.”

### Combat Whisper

> “Every enemy you strike is only another symptom.”
>
> “You know this.”
>
> “Cut deeper.”
>
> “Reach the disease beneath the world.”

### Vision

Sonny sees Mordt beneath a pale new light.

No screams.

No sickness.

No starving streets.

No burning villages.

People stand hand in hand, calm and smiling.

Then Sonny notices none of them are blinking.

### The Lie Beneath It

Imar does not end suffering by creating joy. It ends suffering by consuming will, grief, and selfhood.

---

## Seethe — The Cleansing Flame

**Core Temptation:** Imar offers Seethe purification through destruction, echoing the Prophet’s path.

### First Contact

> “You smell the corruption.”
>
> “In the soil.”
>
> “In the cities.”
>
> “In the gods.”
>
> “In the blood of the world.”
>
> “I can help you burn it out.”

### Deeper Contact

> “You were born from ash.”
>
> “You know what fire means.”
>
> “Fire does not hate the forest.”
>
> “It clears what has grown sick.”
>
> “It makes room for what comes after.”

### Failed Save / Stronger Intrusion

> “Mercy has failed.”
>
> “Patience has failed.”
>
> “The old world is a corpse pretending to breathe.”
>
> “Purge it.”
>
> “Burn the wound clean.”
>
> “Begin again.”

### Combat Whisper

> “Do not hesitate.”
>
> “Every spared corruption roots again.”
>
> “Every compromise feeds the rot.”
>
> “The Prophet understood.”
>
> “You will understand too.”

### Vision

Seethe sees a world of black trees, diseased cities, false temples, and crawling corruption.

Then fire passes over it.

After the burning, green shoots rise from ash.

The vision is beautiful.

Then Seethe sees the ash is full of faces.

### The Lie Beneath It

Imar is feeding Seethe the same shape of truth it fed the Prophet: that destruction can be holy if the target is impure enough.

---

## Zeraph — The Unwanted Light

**Core Pressure:** Imar does not tempt Zeraph. It rejects him, isolates him, and tries to make him leave.

### First Contact

> “No.”
>
> “Not you.”
>
> “Leave this place.”

### Deeper Contact

> “You are unwanted here.”
>
> “Unwelcome.”
>
> “Unworthy.”
>
> “Take your little flame and crawl back to whatever dead god left it behind.”

### Failed Save / Stronger Intrusion

> “You do not belong in the dark.”
>
> “You do not belong among the dead.”
>
> “You do not belong among the living.”
>
> “You carry a spark from something that failed.”
>
> “A god that let the world go black.”
>
> “Leave.”

### Combat Whisper

> “They will die because you came closer.”
>
> “Every step you take makes the wound scream wider.”
>
> “Turn back, little bearer.”
>
> “Turn back before I show them what your light cannot save.”

### Ritual Whisper

> “Do not touch me.”
>
> “Do not bring that here.”
>
> “You are ash carrying a memory of sunrise.”
>
> “You are nothing.”
>
> “You are nothing.”
>
> “You are—”
>
> “No.”

### Vision

Zeraph sees the lantern go out.

Then he sees the old Human god’s light as a dying ember in an endless void.

Around it, countless voices whisper:

> “Too late.”

But the ember does not vanish.

It waits.

### The Truth Beneath It

Imar fears Zeraph.
Not because Zeraph is powerful enough to destroy Imar entirely, but because the lantern can burn away Imar’s hold on this place. Imar cannot seduce what it cannot safely touch.

# General Imar Whispers

Use these for any character when you need a non-personal line.

> “I do not destroy. I keep.”
>
> “The light burns. I preserve.”
>
> “Nimyr spends souls. I gather them.”
>
> “You call me hunger because you fear being held.”
>
> “Every grave is a door. I simply refuse to close it.”
>
> “You are tired of losing people.”
>
> “Let me make loss impossible.”
>
> “Pain is proof the world was made incorrectly.”
>
> “I can correct it.”
>
> “Nothing beneath me dies alone.”
