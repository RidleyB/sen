## Back to the Safehouse

The party returns to the safehouse outside Blacktide.

Riot is present when they arrive. Kael’ith returns shortly afterward, having been watching the town from a distance and keeping track of Imperial movements.

What Kael’ith reports:

* Imperials are **actively searching for fugitives** — likely the escaped prisoners from the Oubliette.
* Dockworkers are being questioned and checked against descriptions.
* Patrols have doubled around the harbor.
* **All ships are being inspected before departure.**

### Involving Kael’ith and Riot

The party may choose whether to bring Kael’ith and Riot with them.

Possible choices:

**If they involve them normally:**

* Kael’ith insists Riot leave Karthold with the party.
* Riot agrees reluctantly.
* Both accompany the party toward the docks.

**If the party suggests splitting up:**

* Kael’ith refuses to abandon Riot in Blacktide.
* If the party insists, Kael’ith will escort Riot separately toward the docks and attempt to meet them there.

**If the party attempts to leave without them entirely:**

* Riot will not remain behind willingly.
* Kael’ith attempts to catch up with the party during the dock scene.

The intended outcome is that **Riot reaches the Pale Wench**, even if the party initially leaves him behind.

## Veylan’s Position

Captain Veylan is still honoring his agreement.

Once the party delivers the **lockbox**, he will take them off Karthold.

However, the ship cannot simply depart immediately.

Departure is blocked because:

* Imperial inspectors are checking ships
* The harbor master has halted outbound traffic
* Soldiers are patrolling the docks

The Pale Wench can leave if one of the following occurs:

* the inspection line collapses due to chaos
* the crew casts off quickly before the Imperials react
* the party forces the issue

Veylan is currently **stalling the inspection team**.

## Crossing Blacktide

To reach the docks, the party must move through the town.

Atmosphere of Blacktide:

* Imperial patrols stopping civilians
* Lanterns hung across streets and docks
* Whistles occasionally signaling patrol movements
* Suspicious dockworkers watching strangers carefully
* Fishing crews whispering about soldiers searching for someone

The town feels **tense and alert**, but not yet fully locked down.

## Redwake & Blackfish Smuggler Loyalists

Redwake’s betrayal still echoes through the town.

Some smugglers loyal to Redwake or the Blackfish crew remain in Blacktide.

During the crossing, roll **1d6**.

On a **1–2**, someone recognizes the party.

Possible NPCs:

* a wounded smuggler who survived the warehouse confrontation
* a lookout who saw them near the warehouse earlier
* a Blackfish smuggler loyal to Redwake

They do **not attack**.

Instead they shout loudly to draw attention.

> “Those are the bastards!”

This immediately attracts nearby Imperial soldiers.

Stealth transitions into a **chase scene**.

## The Vincularis

Among the Imperial forces in Blacktide is a Vincularis attached to the Imperial expedition.

Creed is distinctive and easily recognized:

* black ceremonial robes
* blindfold covering the eyes
* a faint red sigil glowing beneath the chest
* ritual chains and insignia marking Imperial authority

Dockworkers give her a wide berth.

If the party sees her, it is clear she is **not a normal soldier**.

## Urban Chase (If Spotted)

If the party is exposed or raises too much suspicion, run a short pursuit.

Run **3 rounds of chase checks** before reaching the docks.

Each round players may attempt:

* Athletics
* Acrobatics
* Stealth
* Deception
* Intimidation

DC **13**.

Failure consequences may include:

* crossbow bolt grazes someone (1d6 damage)
* market stall overturned, slowing the group
* patrol whistles signaling reinforcements
* soldiers closing distance

If the party succeeds overall, they reach the docks **ahead of the soldiers**.

If they fail repeatedly, Imperials arrive at the pier shortly after them.

## The Pale Wench

When the party reaches the docks:

The Pale Wench is **still tied to the pier**.

Crew members rush across the deck preparing lines and sails while Captain Veylan loudly argues with an Imperial inspection team.

Composition of the inspection team:

* 1 Imperial officer
* 1 Vincularis (Creed)
* 5 Imperial soldiers

They are questioning the crew and preparing to inspect cargo.

Veylan is deliberately **stalling them**.

## The Dock Fight

If violence breaks out, the objective is **not to defeat the Imperials**.

The objective is to **launch the ship before reinforcements arrive**.

Actions that can be taken:

* cut mooring lines
* shove the ship from the pier
* raise sails
* fight boarding soldiers
* push dock planks away

Each round the crew completes **one launch task**.

After **3 rounds**, the Pale Wench pulls free of the dock.

Imperial reinforcements begin arriving from the town shortly afterward.

## Kael’ith’s Sacrifice

As the ship begins to pull away, the Vincularis identifies Riot aboard the deck.
The Vincularis raises a hand and begins a lethal spell aimed directly at Riot.
Kael’ith recognizes what is happening instantly.
Without hesitation, he steps between Riot and the spell.
The magic strikes him instead.
The effect is immediate and brutal.
Kael’ith disintegrates where he stands.
The ship pulls away from the pier as the ash of him scatters across the deck.
Riot watches in stunned silence.

## Act IV — The Harbor Chase

Moments after the Pale Wench clears the dock, a horn sounds across the harbor.
Through the mist emerges an **Imperial pursuit vessel**.
The cutter turns sharply toward the fleeing ship and begins closing distance.
The chase begins.

## Ship Chase Sequence

Captain Veylan attempts a dangerous maneuver.

He intends to steer the Pale Wench through waters where a predator known to stalk the bay hunts.

A **Brine Howler** — a massive serpentine predator of the deep.

Description:

* long eel-like body
* crocodilian armored plates
* shark-like skull
* dark gray hide that blends with black waves

If Veylan navigates correctly, the creature may attack the pursuing cutter instead.

Run this as a **5-round skill challenge**.

Goal:

**4 successes before 3 failures**

Players contribute actions from the deck.

Possible roles:

### Helm

Survival or Water Vehicles
Navigating currents and shoals.

### Sail Handling

Athletics
Adjusting rigging and sails to gain speed.

### Lookout

Perception
Spotting hazards, currents, or shortcuts.

### Arcane / Creative

Spells influencing wind, fog, or water.
DC **13**.

### Chase Events (Roll Each Round)

1. Crosswind gust — sails must be corrected or speed is lost
2. Hidden shoal — risky maneuver required
3. Imperial ballista shot — Dexterity save or hull damage
4. Dense fog bank — opportunity to break line of sight
5. Falling rigging — Athletics check to repair quickly
6. Favorable current — clever use grants automatic success

### Victory Condition

If the party reaches **4 successes first**:
The Pale Wench slips clear of the creature’s territory.
The Brine Howler turns on the pursuing cutter instead.
The Imperial vessel is forced to defend itself while the party escapes.

### Failure Condition

If **3 failures occur first**:
The cutter closes the distance.
Prepare for **naval boarding combat** while the Brine Howler circles both ships.

## Final Scene

The Pale Wench finally clears the harbor.

Behind them:

* the Imperial cutter
* the Brine Howler
* the burning lanterns of Blacktide

The city shrinks into mist.

Riot stands silently at the rail.

Kael’ith is gone.
