## Collapse into the Caves

Beyond the raised guard post, the corridor narrows sharply. Chunks of masonry and jagged beams form a jagged barricade. The air is thick, musty, and heavy with dust and smoke. You can feel the faint tremor of the earlier explosion in your bones, a persistent hum of instability.

As you step forward, the floor gives way beneath you. Stone and rubble tumble away. You lurch, catching yourself on a broken beam, and realize that the prison itself has fractured — the walls ahead are gone entirely, opening into a **dark cavity below**.

Cold, damp air rushes upward, carrying the scent of earth and mineral, a stark contrast to the iron and rot of the prison. Water trickles faintly from somewhere unseen, echoing through the chamber with an irregular drip.

Jagged stone surrounds you on all sides, forming a natural chute leading down into a **subterranean cave system**. Old mining tracks, half-collapsed wooden supports, and abandoned tools hint that this area was once used by laborers long before the prison was built. Shadows twist and stretch in the dim light, making shapes that may or may not be alive.

Faint sounds reach you: the scrape of claw on stone, the drip of water into pools, the distant echo of collapsing tunnels further down. Somewhere below, the faint glimmer of phosphorescent mineral catches your eye, reflecting off slick rock like distant stars.

### Into the Mines

The air grows cooler and wetter as you descend the fractured tunnel. Your boots scrape over broken stone and twisted iron, the remnants of old prison infrastructure jutting from the earth like ribs. Drips echo faintly in the dark, mingling with the slow pulse of water flowing somewhere nearby.

The flicker of light from your torches reveals a network of **rough-hewn tunnels**, clearly **once mined** but long since reclaimed by the dark. You can make out **carved supports**, **rusted rails**, and a **thin channel of greenish water** flowing through one side — **the mine runoff** that snakes deeper into the rock.

### Branching Paths

As the party progresses, they come upon a **junction of caverns** — several branching channels twisting away from the main runoff.

* The **central path** follows the **runoff**, winding slowly **upward** toward fresher air — the **way out**.
* The **side paths** descend further into **dark, uncharted tunnels**, where the echoes are deeper and the air turns stale.
* Small bones, scraps of fur, and claw marks along the stone walls suggest the **beastmen** prowl the lower tunnels.

If players investigate or wander off the main path, they’ll sense:

The faint sound of breathing. Wet. Animal.
The scrape of bone on stone.

It’s clear that **further in means danger**, while **following the runoff** feels faintly cleaner, fresher — a lifeline toward freedom.

## The Acid Path

**Acid Runoff Route**

### The Runoff Chamber

**Player-Facing Description**
The stone passage opens into a broad chamber choked with steam. The air burns your throat. Above, rusted sluice pipes and warped metal troughs constantly drip thin streams of acidic runoff that sizzle on impact with the stone. Portions of the floor have dissolved into shallow pits of cloudy liquid. Metal walkways that once crossed the room have partially collapsed, hanging crooked from their anchors. Every surface looks unstable. As you step inside, the ceiling bulges and quivers. Then shapes begin to drop, splattering onto the floor with wet, corrosive hisses.

**DM Notes**

* **Oozes:** A cluster of gray oozes clings to the ceiling around the central runoff point. Entering the room dislodges them. Have two drop immediately, and one more drop on round two.
* **Terrain:**

  * **Acid Pools:** Difficult terrain. Any creature that moves through takes minor acid damage.
  * **Collapsed Walkway:** Creatures moving across it must pass a simple check or fall into an acid pit, taking the same chip damage.
  * **Sluice Drip Zone:** Crossing beneath an active sluice exposes the creature to a single instance of light corrosion damage.
* **Structural Instability:** Loud impacts, heavy spells, or significant weight can dislodge debris. Use this as soft pressure, not lethal.
* **Tactical Tone:** This isn’t just a combat encounter; it’s a hazard zone where positioning matters. Encourage movement, but punish careless movement.

### The Squeeze Corridor

**Player-Facing Description**
The runoff chamber narrows into a long stone throat. The ceiling dips low, the floor pitched slightly toward a rusted grate in the center. A thick, slow-moving smear of gray matter shivers at the far end, and another at the entrance behind you shifts toward the sound of your steps. They advance with dreadful inevitability, pressing the corridor into a tightening passage of slick stone and ooze. You spot a metal lever set into a wall recess halfway down the corridor, though the mechanism behind it looks fused with corrosion and mineral crust. 

**DM Notes**

* **Two Oozes:** One behind the party, one ahead. Both begin advancing once the players commit to the corridor.
* **Primary Challenge:** Pressure. They are slow, but the corridor is narrow, slick, and only offers a few choices.
* **The Lever:**

  * Pulling it activates the grate.
  * The mechanism is partially fused; require a strength check or creative tool use.
  * Once freed, pulling the lever causes the floor grate to drop open.
  * Any ooze on it falls through into the drainage void below.
  
* **Creative Options:**
  * Climb into the low ceiling struts.
  * Use objects from the runoff room to jam or distract an ooze.
  * Force the ooze onto the collapsing metal walkway fragment they carried or shoved into the corridor.
  * Scrape through the narrowest point with checks, taking grazing acid damage.
   
* **Combat Path:**
  * Straight fight is possible but taxing in a confined space.
  * Engaging one ooze delays long enough for the other to catch up.
  
* **Environmental Pressure:**
  * The corridor floor is lightly coated with acidic runoff. Treat moving more than half speed as minor chip damage unless boots or magic prevent contact.
  * The walls are slick. Failed maneuver checks result in momentary restraint or acid splash.

## Blighted Path

### The Ambush of Thorns

**Player-Facing Description**
The cavern floor narrows into a grove of twisted, dark thorns, half-rotted yet pulsating with a subtle malevolence. The air is thick with rot. 

Seethe suddenly stiffens, ears flat, then trembles as a pulse runs through the earth beneath your feet. A low, guttural howl echoes from deeper in the dark, followed by the snapping of twigs and guttural cries—the Beastmen are nearby. The briars shiver unnaturally, as if alive. Any careless step risks giving them notice.

**DM Notes**

* **Blighted Briars:** Animate and lash out if players touch the thorns or trigger noise. Treat them as swarms that grapple and restrain with minor acid/poison or necrotic damage over time.
* **Avoidance Opportunity:**

  * Players can attempt a Knowledge (Beastman Curse) or History check to recognize patterns of Beastman territory and the blight’s unnatural movement.
  * Success allows careful, quiet movement: stealth through the grove without triggering the briars.
* **Failed Check:**

  * Initiates a combat encounter against 2–3 blighted briar clusters.
  * Beastmen may join if combat drags out, adding urgency.
* **Terrain Effects:**

  * Difficult terrain through tangled roots and thorns.
  * Acidic sap or necrotic residue causes minor chip damage when moving through or being grappled.

### Tomb of the Forgotten Soldier

**Player-Facing Description**
Beyond the briar grove, the cave widens into a hollow chamber choked with thick vines. At the center lies a weathered tomb, half-sunken into the earth, the stone cracked and streaked with dark rot. The surrounding briars are thick, pulsating as if guarding the tomb itself. Inside, a greatsword rests upon the remains of a long-forgotten ascendant soldier of Karthold prison. The blade tang is sturdy, though the hilt is decayed, a whisper of old grandeur. Small treasures lie scattered around the tomb, faded by time yet still worth the risk.

**DM Notes**

* **Briar Miniboss:**

  * Treat the surrounding briars as a single large animate hazard. It can restrain, lash, or ensnare multiple PCs at once.
  * Defeating or circumventing the briar patch requires clever use of environment, magic, or strength.
* **Treasure:**

  * Greatsword: tang in perfect condition, hilt rotten (requires repair to be wieldable).
  * Challenge 0–4 treasure hoard: old coins, minor magical trinkets, or a consumable magical item appropriate for level.
* **Exit Path:**

  * Once the tomb is cleared or bypassed, the path leads to an abandoned mine shaft previously explored.
  * This shaft connects them back toward the coastline, converging with the Acid Runoff Path at the coastal approach.

**Tactical Notes:**

* The combination of terrain and animated briars ensures this is a hazard-focused encounter with combat optional if the players plan carefully.
* Encourage creative solutions: burning, cutting, or magically manipulating the briars rather than straight combat.
* The tomb provides a rewarding discovery, incentivizing players to engage with environmental storytelling rather than simply rushing forward.

## Arrival at Kael’ith’s Safehouse (Session End)

The long climb through the runoff tunnels ends at a break in the stone where cold, salt-heavy wind cuts through the air. You push out through a narrow grate bent by the explosion’s shockwave. Beyond it, the coast opens wide — jagged black cliffs, the distant crash of waves, and the lights of a small port flickering a few kilometers north.

The place is quiet. Cargo cranes sit motionless. Lanterns sway. Even from here you can hear gulls and the low rumble of longshoremen finishing their shifts.

Kael’ith leads you along the cliff edge, keeping low, stopping whenever the wind picks up or patrol torches sweep across the dark. Eventually he brings you to a squat shack half-buried in the stone. Driftwood patched the cracks in the walls. A rusted chimney leans like it’s given up.

He pushes the door open.

Inside: a single room. A battered table. A cold stove. A cot that’s seen too many damp nights. Shelves stacked with loose bandages, salted fish, a few tools, and a worn cloak or two — nothing valuable, everything functional. The smell of sea brine and old oil clings to everything. Kael’ith shuts the door behind you.

He lifts a few floorboard back to reveal his true supplies; weapons, armor, enough to get started.

“You’ll be safe here,” he says. “For tonight. Patrols don’t sweep this stretch often.”

He drops to a knee beside Riot, checking his breathing.

“I’ll keep watch. Rest while you can. Tomorrow, we talk about getting you off this island.”

Outside, the wind batters the boards. Inside, for the first time since the scaffold, the world stops trying to kill you.

**Session ends.**
