# AotF Session 14 — **The Heart Beneath Haldrath**

**Session Function:** Finale of the Sunken Keep dungeon.
**Core Arc:** The party learns the truth of Haldrath, confronts what remains of Commander Vale, and Zeraph’s lantern becomes the first real answer to the Black Blood ichor.

**Recommended Runtime:** 4–5 hours
**Primary Pillars:** Exploration → Revelation → Boss Fight → Ritual Objective → Escape
**Tone:** Tragic, oppressive, sacred horror, then desperate release.

---

## Session Flow

| Scene                           |                                              Purpose |    Runtime |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------: | ---------: |
| 1. The Last Camp                | Discover the sealed survivors and Forbiddance ritual |  30–45 min |

# Scene 1 — The Last Camp

## DM Purpose

This scene should feel like archaeology inside a tomb.

The party should understand that these people were not slaughtered in battle. They were trapped, protected, abandoned, and slowly starved beneath Haldrath.

This is where the haunt becomes tragic instead of merely frightening.

---

## Player-Facing Read-Aloud

> The tunnel slopes downward until the stone gives way to a broad subterranean cavern.
>
> At first, it looks like ruins.
>
> Then the shapes become clearer.
>
> Tents.
>
> Bedrolls.
>
> Cookfires.
>
> Crates.
>
> A camp.
>
> Long rotted canvas hangs in strips from bent supports. Pots sit cold beside circles of ancient ash. Skeletons lie wrapped in blankets, gathered in clusters, or slumped against the cavern walls as though they simply sat down and never rose again.
>
> At the center of the camp, silver-white runes have been carved in a wide circle across the stone floor.
>
> Though the air is wet, black, and foul, the runes remain untouched.
>
> They still glow.

---

## Camp Exploration

Let the party investigate freely. Use these as discoveries, not locked plot gates.

### General Features

* The camp held roughly **forty to sixty people**.
* There are signs of families, soldiers, servants, cooks, and common townsfolk.
* No supplies remain except scraps, bone, rusted tools, and empty water skins.
* The dead were never buried.
* Some bodies were arranged near the ritual circle, as though they were trying to stay close to it.
* A few skeletons bear armor or militia gear, but most were civilians.

## Key Discoveries

### 1. The Ration Wall

A stone wall covered in tally marks.

At first they are neat.

Then cramped.

Then frantic.

Then they stop.

**Read-Aloud**

> Someone carved lines into the stone.
>
> Days, perhaps.
>
> Weeks.
>
> Months.
>
> The first rows are orderly. Then the marks crowd together, cut deeper, overlapping, as though the hand making them had begun to shake.
>
> Beneath them, in a different hand, someone carved only one sentence:
>
> “The light did not come.”

---

### 2. The Paladin’s Body

At the center of the ritual circle lies the armored corpse of the paladin.

His sword is planted point-down before him. His hands are still wrapped around the hilt.

His chest plate is split open from a self-inflicted sacrificial wound.

**Check:** DC 15 Religion or Medicine.

On a success, the party understands: he used his own death as the final component of the ritual.

---

### 3. The Sealed Passage

A collapsed or deliberately sealed tunnel marks where the survivors entered.

**Check:** DC 15 Investigation or Stonecunning-style feature.

On a success, the party learns the passage was sealed from the outside and reinforced from above.

The people below could not reopen it.

---

### 4. The Blackened Water Jars

Several water jars are stained black inside.

**Check:** DC 14 Nature, Arcana, or Medicine.

On a success:

* The water was contaminated before the people entered the camp.
* Someone had been drinking corrupted water before the sealing.
* The contamination predates the survivors’ imprisonment.

This points toward Vale’s corruption beginning **before** the survivors were forced below.

---

## Optional Camp Haunt

Use this if you want a short supernatural moment before the paladin manifests.

### The Starving Dead

As the party disturbs the camp, the dead begin replaying their final days.

Not as combatants. As memories.

**Read-Aloud**

> The air shivers.
>
> For a moment, the dead are not dead.
>
> Pale shapes sit around dead fires. Children lean against their mothers. Soldiers stare at empty ration crates. A woman tries to pour water from a bone-dry skin into a wooden cup.
>
> None of them look at you.
>
> They are not here with you.
>
> They are still trapped in the last days of Haldrath.

**Effect:** Each character must make a **DC 14 Wisdom saving throw**.

On a failure, they take **2d6 psychic damage** and hear one whispered phrase:

* “We were promised rescue.”
* “The Castellan said below was safer.”
* “The light burned above.”
* “The dark sang below.”
* “The paladin held the door shut.”
* “We died where he saved us.”

On a success, no damage, but they still hear one phrase.

This haunt should not begin combat. It is emotional pressure.

# Scene 2 — The Paladin’s Echo

## DM Purpose

The Paladin gives the party:

1. The truth of Haldrath’s final days.
2. The threat of the font below.
3. The suspicion that Nimyr’s conversions are soul-condemnation.
4. The idea that Zeraph’s lantern can do something no one else could.
5. The path forward.

The Paladin should be fragmented. He remembers enough to matter, but not everything.

---

## The Paladin

Use any name you like. Suggested names:

* **Ser Caldran Vey**
* **Ser Aurel Harrow**
* **Ser Mathian Valeguard**
* **Ser Odran Halvek**

For this plan, I’ll use **Ser Caldran Vey**.

He was a paladin sworn to protect Haldrath’s people, not Nimyr, not the keep, not the Castellan.

---

> The runes brighten.
>
> Silver-white light moves through the carved lines like breath through frost.

> The corpse at the center of the circle stirs.

>

>

>

>

## Roleplaying Caldran

**Voice:** Low, tired, reverent, ashamed.
**Manner:** He avoids looking at the dead for too long.
**Memory:** Fragmented around his death and the deepest corruption.
**Emotion:** He does not want absolution. He wants the danger ended.

## What Caldran Does Not Know

Caldran cannot answer everything.

He does **not** know:

* The true cosmic origin of Imar.
* Whether all Black Blood fonts share one mind.
* Whether Nimyr understands Imar.
* Whether the lantern can destroy the ichor permanently.
* What will happen when the font is quelled.

He suspects the font below is only a wound, not the whole disease.

---

## Paladin’s Boon

Before the party leaves, Caldran can bless them.

This gives the party a limited protective resource for the finale.

### **Boon: Last Light of Haldrath**

Caldran raises his sword and releases the remaining strength of the Forbiddance ritual.

Each party member gains **one Last Light die**, a **d6**.

A character may spend this die to add it to:

* A saving throw against fear, charm, corruption, psychic influence, or possession.
* A death saving throw.
* A check made during the collapse escape.
* A check made to protect Zeraph during the lantern ritual.

When Zeraph spends this die on a lantern ritual check, it becomes a **d8**.

Once used, it is gone.

# Scene 3 — Descent to the Font

## DM Purpose

This is the final pressure valve before the fight.

The party should feel the protection of the Forbiddance fade behind them. The ichor becomes active, intimate, and persuasive.

---

## Read-Aloud

> Past the paladin’s ward, the cavern changes.
>
> The stone sweats.
>
> Thin black veins thread through the walls like roots beneath skin.
>
> Water drips from the ceiling, but every drop lands too heavily, thick and dark, striking the stone with a sound like a slow heartbeat.
>
> The air tastes of iron.
>
> Behind you, the silver light of the camp dims.
>
> Ahead, something vast breathes in the dark.

---

## Environmental Hazards

Use two or three on the way down.

### 1. Black Vein Floor

A section of tunnel pulses with black veins.

**Group Check:** DC 14 Acrobatics or Survival.

* Success: The party crosses safely.
* Failure: Each failing character takes **2d6 necrotic damage** and must make a **DC 13 Wisdom saving throw** or hear Imar whisper.

### 2. The Whispering Narrow

The tunnel tightens. Each character hears a personalized promise.

**Saving Throw:** DC 14 Wisdom.

On a failure, the character has disadvantage on their next initiative roll or first saving throw in the boss chamber.

Possible whispers:

* “You could save them all.”
* “You could be forgiven.”
* “You could bring them back.”
* “You could make the pain mean something.”
* “You could stop being afraid.”
* “You could be loved without condition.”

On a success, the character hears the whisper but rejects it.

### 3. The False Water

A black pool blocks the path.

It looks drinkable to anyone who is thirsty, exhausted, wounded, or desperate.

**Check:** DC 15 Insight, Nature, or Arcana.

On failure, a character who touches or drinks from it must make a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw**.

* Success: Take **1d6 necrotic damage**.
* Failure: Take **3d6 necrotic damage** and become **Ichor-Marked** until the next long rest.

### Ichor-Marked Condition

While Ichor-Marked:

* The character hears Imar clearly.
* The first time they hit Vale, Vale regains **10 hit points**.
* They have disadvantage on the first saving throw against Vale’s **Grief Command**.

A character can remove Ichor-Marked with:

* Lesser Restoration
* Protection from Evil and Good
* A successful DC 16 Religion check during a short ritual
* Zeraph touching the lantern to their chest and succeeding on a DC 14 Wisdom or Religion check

# Boss Encounter — Vale, the Tortured Castellan

## Encounter Purpose

This fight should not be “kill the monster and win.”

The true win condition is **quelling the font**.

Vale is the font’s guardian, victim, and weapon.

The party needs to:

1. Survive Vale.
2. Keep the font from overwhelming them.
3. Create an opening for Zeraph.
4. Protect Zeraph while the lantern is thrust into the ichor.
5. Escape once the cavern begins to collapse.

---

## Battlefield Layout

Use a roughly circular cavern with three elevation tiers.

### Zones

| Zone        | Description                                 | Mechanics                                    |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Outer Shelf | Entrance area, broken rocks, old bones      | Mostly safe; difficult terrain in places     |
| Middle Ring | Ichor streams, slick stone, corrupted roots | Difficult terrain; DC 13 Dex save if dashing |
| Inner Basin | Base of the font                            | Heavy corruption; ritual location            |
| Font Itself | Surging black ichor                         | Hazardous; required for lantern ritual       |

---

## Terrain Features

### Ichor Streams

Several 5-foot-wide streams run from the font.

A creature entering an ichor stream or starting its turn there takes **1d6 necrotic damage**.

If the creature is prone, restrained, or unconscious in the ichor, it takes **2d6 necrotic damage** instead.

### Slick Stone

When a creature moves more than half its speed on the Middle Ring, it must succeed on a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** or fall prone.

Characters moving carefully at half speed do not need to save.

### Bone Piles

Bone piles provide half cover.

If struck by Vale’s slam or a font surge, they explode into shrapnel.

Creatures within 5 feet make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** or take **1d6 piercing damage**.

### The Font

The font counts as a cursed magical hazard.

A creature that touches it directly must make a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw**.

* Success: Takes **2d6 necrotic damage**.
* Failure: Takes **4d6 necrotic damage** and is Ichor-Marked.

Zeraph is not immune to this, but the lantern can protect him during the ritual.

---

# Vale’s Boss Mechanics

## Baseline Difficulty

Designed for approximately **4–5 characters around level 6–8**.

If your party is lower level, reduce HP and damage.
If your party is higher level, increase HP and add one extra legendary action.

## Vale’s Traits

### Font-Bound

Vale cannot willingly move more than 30 feet from the font.

If forced beyond this range, black veins drag him 15 feet back toward it at the end of his turn.

### Grief-Driven Regeneration

At the start of Vale’s turn, if he is touching ichor, he regains **10 hit points**.

This regeneration stops for 1 round if:

* Vale takes radiant damage.
* Zeraph uses the lantern within 30 feet of him.
* A character succeeds on a DC 16 Religion or Arcana check as an action to disrupt the ichor around him.

### Broken Humanity

When Vale is reduced below half HP, his human consciousness briefly surfaces.

The party can appeal to him.

As an action, a character may make a **DC 16 Persuasion, Religion, or Insight check**.

On a success, Vale loses one legendary action until the start of his next turn.

On a success by 5 or more, Vale also does not regenerate on his next turn.

Good phrases to reward:

* Reminding him of Haldrath’s people.
* Naming his failure without cruelty.
* Calling him Commander rather than monster.
* Saying the people below deserved rest.
* Saying Nimyr was the enemy, but Imar lied.

---

## Vale’s Actions

### Multiattack

Vale makes two attacks: one **Ichor Claw** and one **Grasping Tendril**.

---

### Ichor Claw

**Melee Weapon Attack:** +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.
**Hit:** 2d8 + 4 slashing damage plus 1d6 necrotic damage.

If the target is Ichor-Marked, add another **1d6 psychic damage**.

---

### Grasping Tendril

**Melee Weapon Attack:** +7 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target.
**Hit:** 1d10 + 4 bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled.

Escape DC: **15**.

Until the grapple ends, the target takes **1d6 necrotic damage** at the start of each of Vale’s turns.

Vale can grapple up to two creatures this way.

---

### Cast Down

Vale hurls one grappled creature up to 20 feet.

The target must make a **DC 15 Dexterity saving throw**.

* Failure: Takes **2d6 bludgeoning damage**, lands prone, and slides 10 feet toward the nearest ichor stream.
* Success: Takes half damage and does not slide.

---

### Grief Command — Recharge 5–6

Vale releases a psychic command through the font.

Each creature of Vale’s choice within 60 feet must make a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**.

On a failure, the creature takes **3d6 psychic damage** and must use its reaction, if available, to move up to half its speed toward the font.

On a success, the creature takes half damage and does not move.

Characters who spend their **Last Light die** roll it and add it to this save.

---

### Black Blood Eruption — Recharge 6

Vale slams both hands into the ground.

Choose three points within 60 feet.

Each creature within 10 feet of one of those points must make a **DC 15 Dexterity saving throw**.

* Failure: **4d6 necrotic damage** and knocked prone.
* Success: Half damage and not prone.

The affected areas become ichor-coated difficult terrain until initiative count 20 of the next round.

---

## Legendary Actions

Vale has **2 legendary actions** per round.

He can use one at the end of another creature’s turn.

### Move

Vale moves up to half his speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

### Tendril Lash

Vale makes one Grasping Tendril attack.

### Font Pulse — Costs 2 Actions

A pulse erupts from the font.

Each creature within 15 feet of the font must make a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw**.

* Failure: **2d6 necrotic damage** and pushed 10 feet away.
* Success: Half damage and not pushed.

If Zeraph is actively performing the lantern ritual, this save is made with advantage while holding the lantern.

---

# Lair Actions

On initiative count 20, losing initiative ties, choose one.

Do not repeat the same lair action two rounds in a row.

## 1. The Font Speaks

One character hears Imar directly.

They must make a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**.

On failure, they are overwhelmed by a personalized vision and have disadvantage on their next attack roll, ability check, or saving throw before the end of their next turn.

## 2. Ichor Surge

A 20-foot line of ichor surges from the font.

Creatures in the line make a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw**.

* Failure: **2d6 necrotic damage** and knocked prone.
* Success: Half damage.

The line remains as an ichor stream.

## 3. Bones of Haldrath

Skeletal hands rise from the ichor.

Up to three creatures standing on the Middle Ring or Inner Basin must make a **DC 14 Strength saving throw**.

On failure, their speed is reduced to 0 until the end of their next turn.

## 4. Vale Remembers

Vale screams a fragment of his memory.

Until initiative count 20 next round, checks made to appeal to Vale’s humanity are made with advantage.

However, Vale’s attacks deal +1d6 psychic damage during this round.

---

# Scene 5 — Zeraph and the Lantern

## DM Purpose

This is the true climax.

Zeraph should realize the fight cannot be won by violence alone.

The lantern does not simply blast the font. It must be carried into the heart of corruption and held there while Imar resists.

---

## Triggering the Realization

Trigger this when one of the following happens:

* Vale reaches half HP.
* A character examines the font.
* Zeraph approaches within 30 feet of the font.
* Vale regenerates for the second time.
* A player asks how to stop the ichor.

---

## Read-Aloud for Zeraph

> The lantern flickers.
>
> Not weakly.
>
> Angrily.
>
> Its flame leans toward the font as though pulled by wind no one else can feel.
>
> The black blood recoils from its light.
>
> Just slightly.
>
> Just enough.
>
> And in that moment, Zeraph understands something without words.
>
> The lantern was never meant to shine from a distance.
>
> It has to be brought to the wound.

---

## Ritual Objective

Zeraph must reach the **Inner Basin** and thrust the lantern into the font.

Once he does, the ritual begins.

The party must protect him while the lantern burns the ichor away.

---

## Lantern Ritual Rules

### Starting the Ritual

Zeraph must be adjacent to the font and use an action to thrust the lantern into the ichor.

When he does, read:

> Zeraph drives the lantern into the black blood.
>
> The cavern screams.
>
> Not Vale.
>
> Not the dead.
>
> The cavern itself.
>
> The ichor rises around the lantern like a hand trying to smother a flame.
>
> For one heartbeat, the light disappears.
>
> Then it burns gold-white beneath the surface.

---

## Ritual Progress

The ritual requires **3 successes before 3 failures**.

Zeraph makes one ritual check at the end of each of his turns while maintaining contact with the font.

### Ritual Check Options

Zeraph chooses one each round:

| Skill             | DC | Use                                                    |
| ----------------- | -: | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Religion          | 15 | Calling on his deity / sacred purpose                  |
| Wisdom Save       | 15 | Withstanding Imar’s psychic pressure                   |
| Constitution Save | 15 | Enduring the physical corruption                       |
| Arcana            | 17 | Understanding the font’s structure                     |
| Persuasion        | 17 | Speaking to Vale’s remaining humanity through the font |

If Zeraph spends his **Last Light die**, add **1d8** instead of 1d6.

---

## Maintaining the Ritual

While performing the ritual:

* Zeraph’s speed is 0.
* He must keep one hand on the lantern.
* He has half cover from the rising light and ichor.
* He has advantage on saving throws caused directly by the font.
* If he is moved away from the font, the ritual pauses but does not reset.
* If he is knocked unconscious, the ritual gains 1 failure.

---

## Ritual Damage

At the start of each of Zeraph’s turns while maintaining the ritual, he takes **2d6 necrotic damage**.

He may reduce this damage by making a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw**.

On success, he takes half damage.

If another character uses their action to help shield him, pray with him, stabilize the lantern, or physically hold him in place, Zeraph has advantage on that turn’s ritual check.

---

## Party Support Actions

Other characters can use their actions to assist the ritual.

| Action              | Check                       | DC | Success                                                            |
| ------------------- | --------------------------- | -: | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Hold Back the Ichor | Athletics                   | 15 | Reduce ritual damage by 1d6 this round                             |
| Ward Zeraph         | Religion or Arcana          | 15 | Zeraph gains +2 to his next ritual check                           |
| Speak to Vale       | Persuasion or Insight       | 16 | Vale cannot target Zeraph until the end of Vale’s next turn        |
| Cut the Tendrils    | Attack Roll vs AC 15        |  — | Remove one tendril or hazard near Zeraph                           |
| Anchor the Lantern  | Sleight of Hand or Survival | 14 | If Zeraph is moved, the ritual does not pause                      |
| Share the Burden    | Constitution Save           | 15 | Helper takes 1d6 necrotic damage; Zeraph reduces his damage by 1d6 |

---

## Imar’s Temptations

Each time Zeraph rolls a ritual check, Imar speaks.

Use these as intrusive whispers:

1. “Your light is so small.”
2. “You could heal the world if you stopped resisting.”
3. “Your god left this place. I did not.”
4. “Give me the lantern. I will make it a sun.”
5. “The dead do not need rest. They need purpose.”
6. “Let me in, and no one will ever be alone again.”
7. “You think this is corruption because you fear transformation.”
8. “Nimyr burns souls. I keep them.”

**Mechanical Effect:** Vale’s regeneration drops to 0 until the start of Zeraph’s next turn.

---

### Second Success

>

>
> Beneath them, water surges.
>
> Real water.
>
> Ancient water.
>
> Buried, poisoned, but not dead.

**Mechanical Effect:** All ichor streams deal half damage. Vale loses one legendary action.

---

### Third Success

> The lantern’s flame vanishes.
>
> For one impossible second, there is only darkness.
>
> Then light erupts from beneath the font.
>
> The black blood ignites from within, burning without smoke, without heat, without mercy.
>
> The scream that follows is vast.
>
> It falls through the stone.
>
> Through the dead.
>
> Through every drop of black blood in the cavern.
>
> Then the font bursts open.
>
> Not with ichor.
>
> With water.

**Mechanical Effect:** The font is quelled. Combat ends unless you want Vale to have a final death moment.

---

## Ritual Failures

### First Failure

The font surges.

All creatures within 20 feet of the font make a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw**.

* Failure: **2d6 necrotic damage**.
* Success: Half damage.

### Second Failure

Imar lashes out.

Zeraph must make a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**.

On failure, he is stunned until the end of his next turn.

A party member adjacent to him may use their reaction to take the psychic backlash instead, suffering **3d6 psychic damage** and preventing the stun.

### Third Failure

The lantern is nearly smothered.

Zeraph immediately takes **4d6 necrotic damage** and the ritual pauses.

To resume, another character must spend an action succeeding on a **DC 16 Religion, Arcana, or Medicine check** to clear ichor from the lantern and stabilize him.

The ritual does not fully fail unless the party retreats or Zeraph dies.

# Escape Skill Challenge

## Structure

The party must achieve **6 successes before 3 failures**.

Each character should contribute once before anyone repeats a skill, unless the fiction strongly supports it.

Base DC: **15**

Raise to DC 17 for especially risky actions.
Lower to DC 13 for clever use of resources, maps, spells, rope, prior scouting, or strong narrative positioning.

---

## Failure Consequences

Failures should hurt, but keep the escape moving.

Each failed check causes one:

| Failure Result | Effect                                               |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Falling Stone  | Character takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage               |
| Flood Surge    | Character takes 1d6 bludgeoning and is knocked prone |
| Lost Ground    | Party needs one additional success                   |
| Ichor Backwash | Character makes DC 14 Con save or takes 2d6 necrotic |
| Separated      | One character is cut off and needs rescue            |
| Exhaustion     | Character gains one level of exhaustion after escape |

At **3 total failures**, the party still escapes, but with a major cost:

* Someone loses important gear.
* Someone is trapped and must be rescued in a final check.
* A character gains a lingering injury.
* The exit collapses behind them permanently.
* The lantern is dimmed and cannot be used again until restored.
* An NPC, spirit, or relic is lost.

Avoid killing a character outright from the skill challenge unless they choose an obvious sacrificial action.

# Rewards

The party learns:

* Nimyr offered Haldrath conversion or annihilation.
* Nimyr smote the city with radiant destruction from above.
* The Dawnspire is forged from soul-metal.

Choose what fits your campaign pacing.

A vial of water from the purified source.

**Effect:** As an action, a creature may drink it or pour it over a target.

It can do one of the following:

* End Ichor-Marked.

Single use.

A broken piece of the paladin’s sword remains after the haunt fades.

Can be reforged, socketed into a holy symbol, or used in a future ritual.

**Property:** Once, when used during a ritual against undead, fiends, aberrations, or Black Blood corruption, it grants advantage on the central check.

A cracked commander’s signet from old Haldrath.

Useful as:

# DM Notes for Running the Finale

## Keep the Fight Objective-Based

Do not let the encounter become a static slugfest.

Every round should pressure one of these questions:

* Can Zeraph reach the font?
* Can the party keep Vale away from him?
* Can they stop the regeneration?
* Can they resist Imar’s voice?
* Can they finish before the room becomes unmanageable?

---

## Let the Players Be Clever

Reward:

* Radiant magic.
* Protection from Evil and Good.
* Turn Undead-like effects.
* Spells that move allies.
* Rope, pitons, shields, grapples, Help actions.
* Emotional appeals to Vale.
* Using Caldran’s boon.
* Using water purification magic creatively.

---

## Vale Should Feel Tragic, Not Evil

Good Vale lines during combat:

* “I sealed the doors.”
* “I heard them praying.”
* “I was going to save them.”
* “The light killed them above.”
* “The dark promised they would endure.”
* “Do not make me remember.”
* “Do not take them from me.”
* “They are mine to protect.”
* “No. No, I know this is wrong. I know—”
* “Below was safe. I told them below was safe.”

Imar speaking through Vale:

* “Grief is ownership.”
* “Love is hunger with a kinder name.”
* “He gave them to us.”
* “We kept what Nimyr would have burned.”
* “All souls are used. We are only honest.”

# Scaling

## Easier Party / Lower Level

Use these changes:

* Vale HP: **105–120**
* Vale AC: **15**
* Save DCs: reduce by 1
* Remove one legendary action
* Black Blood Eruption deals **3d6** instead of 4d6
* Ritual requires **3 successes before 4 failures**

## Stronger Party / Higher Level

Use these changes:

* Vale HP: **170–190**
* Vale AC: **17**
* Save DCs: increase by 1
* Vale has **3 legendary actions**
* Add one lair action per round after the ritual begins
* Ritual damage becomes **3d6 necrotic**
* Vale’s regeneration cannot be stopped by radiant damage alone; radiant only reduces it by half unless paired with a successful Religion/Arcana check
