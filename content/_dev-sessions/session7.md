Level 2 means the danger comes from:
* Poison pressure
* Fire control
* Drag mechanics
* Faction chaos
* Party hidden inside or along the warehouse interior.
* Border lanterns extinguished.
* Only central bonfire remains.
* 12 smugglers outside, panicking.
* Dense, dry bramble forest.
* True darkness beyond 40 ft.

The party is unseen. Smugglers assume Beastmen caused the sabotage.

This is a **dynamic 3-faction conflict**:

| Faction   | Goal                                  |
| --------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Beastmen  | Drag prey into darkness               |
| Smugglers | Survive and relight perimeter         |
| Party     | Unknown — stealth, intervene, exploit |

## 1. Starving Beastmen

AC 13<br>
HP 11<br>
+4 to hit<br>
1d6+2 slashing<br>
Advantage in dim light or darkness<br>

**Bonus Action:** Hook and Drag<br>
On hit, target must succeed STR save DC 11 or be pulled 10 ft.

They disengage into darkness after attacking.

## 2. Slobbering Beastmen (Poison Variant)

AC 12<br>
HP 13<br>
+4 to hit<br>
1d6+2 piercing<br>

**On hit:**
Target must succeed CON save DC 11
or become **Infected**:

**Infected Condition**

* Disadvantage on next attack roll
* Speed reduced by 10 ft for 1 round

If target fails 2 infection saves in same combat:
They become Poisoned for 1 minute.

These are extremely dangerous at level 2. Use 2–3 only.

## 3. The Boss — The Horned Ravager

Large Beastman.
It walks hunched but stands taller than the others.
Horns are massive and split. Its spine is overgrown with calcified ridges. One arm is longer than the other.
It breathes like a bull.

### Stats (Level 2 Appropriate But Deadly)

AC 14<br>
HP 55<br>
Speed 40 ft<br>
Resistant to nonmagical weapon damage while in dim light or darkness.<br>

### Abilities

**Gore Charge (Recharge 5–6)**<br>
Moves at least 20 ft in straight line.<br>
+5 to hit.<br>
2d8+3 piercing.<br>
Target must succeed STR save DC 13 or be knocked prone.<br>

**If target is prone in dim light:**
It makes one bonus claw attack.<br>

**Thrash and Scatter (1/Turn)**
**When reduced below half HP:**<br>
It makes a free 10 ft shove attempt against all creatures within 5 ft.

**Crush the Ember**<br>
**If adjacent to a fire source:**
As action, attempt to stomp it out.<br>
DC 13 check.<br>
On success, fire dims one stage.<br>

### Behavior

*The Ravager:*
* Attacks the bonfire initially.

*The Beastmen:*
* Targets isolated prey.
* Attacks whoever is farthest from fire.
* Does not retreat unless below 10 HP.

When it drops a creature:
It attempts to drag 15 ft toward darkness.

## Smugglers

They do not know about the party. Each round roll 1d4:

1 – 2 smugglers attempt to relight a border lantern<br>
2 – 2 smugglers fire crossbows into darkness randomly<br>
3 – 3 form defensive ring around bonfire<br>
4 – 1 attempts to flee to warehouse door<br>

**If 5+ smugglers die:**<br>
Morale breaks.
They attempt full retreat into warehouse.

**If Ravager enters bright light:**<br>
Half the smugglers focus fire on it.

## Escalation Timeline

**Round 1–2:**
Stalkers harass.

**Round 3:**
Gnashers arrive from opposite treeline.

**Round 4:**
The Horned Ravager crashes through bramble.

**Describe:**<br>
Branches snapping.<br>
Ground shaking.<br>
Bonfire flickering.<br>

## Fire System (Critical Mechanic)

**Bonfire has 3 stages:** Bright → Dim → Flickering → Out
* Wind event every 2 rounds (50% chance dims fire).
* Ravager can stomp it.
* Smugglers can feed it (action restores 1 stage).

**If fire goes out:**
All Beastmen gain advantage until end of next round.

## Three-Way Chaos Opportunities

**The party can:**
* Shoot from warehouse windows unnoticed.
* Let both sides weaken each other.
* Open doors suddenly and create new light.
* Sabotage smugglers mid-combat.
* Ignite oil barrels to create bright zones.

Smugglers will not immediately identify party as threat if they intervene against Beastmen.

**But if Beastmen fall first:**
Smugglers turn hostile quickly.

## Victory Paths

### 1. Beastmen Routed

Ravager dies or fire remains Bright 3 rounds after it is bloodied.<br>
Remaining Beastmen scatter.<br>
Smugglers exhausted, wounded.<br>

**Party may:**
* Negotiate
* Ambush
* Interrogate

### 2. Smugglers Slaughtered

**If 8+ smugglers die:**<br>
Beastmen begin dragging bodies instead of fighting.<br>
Party must fight weakened Beastmen alone.<br>
Ravager remains primary threat.<br>

## 3. Beastmen Crushed by Smugglers

Unlikely unless party assists heavily.

**If Ravager dies early:**<br>
Remaining Beastmen disengage.

Smugglers then investigate sabotage.

If they discover party:
Second combat begins immediately.

## Difficulty Control Knobs (Use Quietly)

**To increase difficulty:**
* Add one extra Gnasher.
* Have Ravager focus a single PC relentlessly.
* Dim fire via wind early.

**To reduce difficulty:**
* Remove resistance.
* Reduce Ravager HP to 45.
* Have smugglers deal meaningful crossbow damage.

## Skill Challenge: The Burning Bramble

**Trigger:** Only if warehouse fire spreads into forest.<br>
**Goal:** Escape the wildfire and reach Blacktide with the lockbox.<br>
**Structure:**
* 5 successes before 3 failures
* Each PC must contribute at least once before anyone repeats a skill
* DC 12 standard, DC 14 for higher-risk maneuvers
* Carrying the lockbox imposes disadvantage on one check per round unless two characters coordinate

Failure does not mean death.
It means consequences.

### Base Environmental Effects (Active Entire Challenge)

* Visibility reduced to 30 ft
* Ranged attacks beyond that at disadvantage
* Each round, everyone makes DC 10 CON save or gains 1 level of **Smoke Strain**

  * At 2 stacks: disadvantage on Perception
  * At 3 stacks: speed reduced by 10 ft
  * Clears after short rest in clean air

## Random Disaster Table

Roll 1d6 at the start of each round.

### 1 — Fireline Collapse

A wall of flame drops from burning bramble ahead.

**Options:**

* Athletics (DC 14) to push through narrow gap
* Survival (DC 12) to find alternate route
* Use water/cloth to create safe passage

**Failure:**
1d6 fire damage + 1 Smoke Strain

### 2 — Falling Timber

A burning tree cracks and falls. Dex save DC 13<br>

**Failure:**
2d6 bludgeoning damage <br>
Restrained until freed (Athletics DC 12)

**If carrying lockbox when hit:**<br>
Box is dropped and must be retrieved.

### 3 — Smoke Pocket

Thick smoke rolls low across the ground.

* Constitution DC 13 to push through
* Investigation DC 12 to find higher ground pocket
* Spell use (wind, minor illusion, etc.) creative solutions allowed

**Failure:**
Gain 2 Smoke Strain

### 4 — Animal Stampede

Burning deer or boars charge blindly.

* Animal Handling DC 12 to redirect
* Acrobatics DC 13 to dodge
* Intimidation DC 14 to scatter

**Failure:**<br>
Knocked prone + 1d6 damage

**On critical failure:**<br>
Lockbox carrier knocked down.

### 5 — Fire Jump

Wind shifts.<br>
Fire leaps behind them.<br>
Escape path narrowing.<br>

**Group check:**<br>
At least half must succeed Athletics or Survival DC 12.

**Failure:**<br>
They are partially encircled.<br>
+1 automatic failure on challenge.<br>

## Creative Skill Uses (Encourage These)

* Athletics: Breaking through bramble
* Acrobatics: Moving through falling debris

* Persuasion/Intimidation: Coordinating group movement
