## Overview

This session focuses on investigation, social pressure, and slow escalation. The party works to uncover who stole Captain Veylan Mirth’s lockbox, navigating crew tension, smuggler politics, and the risk of imperial attention. Combat is optional but possible. The session is designed to resolve in approximately **3–4 hours**, ending before the lockbox is physically recovered.

---

## Starting State

* The party is in **Blacktide**, having agreed to help Captain Veylan Mirth in exchange for passage off Karthold.
* Kael’ith has returned to the safehouse to remain with Riot.
* The party is known in town but not trusted.
* The lockbox is missing; only the captain and his crew knew of its existence.

Atmosphere:

* Fog off the water in the morning
* Dock activity already underway
* Locals watch quietly; sailors whisper

---

## Act I — Tension on the Piers

### Visible Signs

* Crew members of *The Pale Bitch* linger around the docks.
* Conversations stop when the party approaches.
* **Boatswain Grit** is notably absent.

### Player Opportunities

* Casual conversation with sailors
* Observing crew behavior
* Visiting taverns or shops to listen rather than interrogate

### Information Gained

* The crew has been tense for days.
* Veylan has been unusually protective of something.
* Some crew believe outsiders will bring imperial trouble.

---

## Act II — The Warehouse

### Location

A salt-stained warehouse along the pier, still nominally under Veylan’s control.

### Findings

* Crates have been shifted recently.
* Drag marks indicate a heavy object was removed.
* Scorching and warped metal suggest a failed attempt to tamper with a magically sealed container.

### The Ledger

The warehouse ledger reveals:

* A crate was moved **off-record** two nights ago.
* The shorthand mark used does not match any of Veylan’s crew.
* The mark is associated with dock runners tied to the **Blackship Runners**.

Implication: the theft involved outside help.

---

## Act III — Smuggler Politics

### Gathering Rumors

In taverns and along the docks, the party may learn:

* The **Saltiron Cartel** and **Blackship Runners** avoid open conflict.
* The Runners have recently refused a job involving a sealed container.
* Someone paid a runner to move something inland and never followed up.

These rumors point toward panic, not profit.

---

## Act IV — Boatswain Grit

### Encounter

Grit is hostile and openly contemptuous of the party.

### What Grit Says

* Outsiders attract imperials.
* Veylan is hiding something dangerous.
* If Blacktide burns, it won’t be his fault.

### What Grit Knows (but resists sharing)

* The lockbox was not opened.
* It was moved inland.
* A dock-runner named **Ashkel “Redwake” Var** was involved.

Grit did **not** steal the lockbox. He is withholding information deliberately.

### Grit’s Agenda

If the situation escalates or the party draws attention, Grit intends to quietly tip off an imperial contact in Harrivale.

---

## Act V — The Culprit Revealed

Through investigation and inference, the party can determine:

* The lockbox was stolen by a **junior quartermaster** affiliated with the Blackship Runners.
* The thief believed it was valuable, not dangerous.
* After failing to open it, they panicked.
* The lockbox is hidden inland in a **collapsed ore storehouse**, awaiting retrieval.

Complication:

* Beastmen activity nearby has delayed recovery.
* No one wants to touch the box anymore.

---

## Session End Point (Recommended)

End the session once the party knows:

* Who stole the lockbox
* Who helped move it
* Where it likely is
* That only Captain Veylan knows what is inside
* That Grit is willing to involve the Imperials

Night falls over Blacktide. Lanterns flicker along the piers. Somewhere inland, a sealed object hums faintly in the dark.

The lockbox has not yet been recovered.

---

## Next Session Hooks

* Recover the lockbox quietly
* Confront the thief directly
* Inform Captain Veylan immediately
* Deal with Grit before he acts

Each choice alters Blacktide’s stability and the party’s chances of leaving Karthold cleanly.
