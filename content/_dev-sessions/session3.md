## Overview

Kael’ith brings the party to Blacktide with the promise of a smuggler captain he once worked with: **Captain Veylan Mirth**, Captain of the Pale Bitch.

But the original deal only covers **Kael’ith and Riot**, not the rest of the party. Veylan is willing to renegotiate—but only if the party proves their value. Meanwhile, part of his crew wants nothing to do with bringing outsiders aboard and begins plotting behind his back.

### The Town’s Reaction

The party arrives to:
* Suspicious stares
* Closed shutters
* Quiet, hurried movements
* A sense of simmering tension

Smugglers and sailors watch them with calculating eyes, sizing up what trouble they represent.

### Meeting Captain Veylan Mirth

Location: **The Old Keelhouse** backroom.

**Veylan’s Deal:**
Kael’ith paid for two spots—Riot and himself.
To get the rest of you aboard, Veylan wants something done:

**THE JOB:**
Retrieve something stolen from him:

* A **sealed lead strongbox**
* Presumed to be taken by a rival smuggler cell, the Salt-Iron Cartel.
* Now believed to be hidden **somewhere in or near Blacktide**

Veylan refuses to say what’s in it, only that it isn’t dangerous, and won't get them in any more trouble than they're already in. He doesn’t know exactly where the box is, and wants the party to do the digging.

## Information Avenues

### 1. Gossip Gathering

* **Scuttle’s Lantern** (sailors, dock workers)
* **Old Keelhouse** (quiet travelers, old-timers)
* **Cask & Kelp** (locals asking questions about who's buying gear)
* **Gannet’s Trove** (Eldeen hears everything and trades in rumors as much as trinkets)

**Successful Leading Rumors Include:**

* The rival smugglers were *seen near the abandoned ore warehouse*.
* Someone matching their description paid a local to *“hold a package”* but never returned.
* A fight broke out between two sailors on **Pier 3** over a *sealed metal box*.
* An Ascendant scout was spotted *surveying the watchtower* two days ago—did they get wind of the box?

Each rumor can be true, false, or half-true depending on pacing.

---

### 2. Direct Investigation Sites

**A. Abandoned Ore Warehouse**

* Guarded by the Salt-Iron Cartel
* Rotted crates- old gear. Loot!
* Marks of something heavy recently dragged.
* Possible trap (rigged shelf collapse, tripwire).

**B. Blacktide Piers (Piers 3 & 4)**

* Pier 3: drunken sailors, broken planks, the collapsed pier
* Pier 4: smuggler-controlled; guarded entry; hint of internal disagreement
* Evidence that someone unloaded cargo here in a hurry

**C. Watchtower Ruin**

* Birds nesting
* Old rope harnesses
* Burnt wax from a lantern
* Scrap of cloth with Veylan’s colors (plant from mutinous crew)

## Mutinous Crew Subplot

Boatswain Keldran believes:

* Outsiders = imperial bait
* Bringing fugitives aboard risks the crew
* Veylan is compromising the ship

He secretly gathers supporters (3–7 crew members):

**If not stopped, they plan to:**

* Alert the **Imperial garrison in Harrivale**
* Sell the location of freed prisoners

### **Key Scenes**

### 1. Confrontation at the Warehouse (Combat Optional)

If the party tracks them:

* The mutineers are meeting in an old warehouse
* They’re just about to send a runner to Harrivale
* If confronted → **combat with sailors**
* If eavesdropped → the party learns the plan
* If stealth is used → they can sabotage the meeting or take out the runner

---

### 2. Veylan’s Reaction

If told:

* Furious
* Disowns Keldran’s faction
* Doubles down on the job:
  **“Get me my strongbox, and I’ll take you across the sea myself.”**

If not told:
* He finds out when Ascendants march into Blacktide later

## Finding the Box

You choose the actual location based on pacing:

### **Option A — Ore Warehouse**

Partially collapsed, dangerous terrain, rival smuggler camp.

### **Option B — Watchtower**

Stolen box stored under loose stones; guarded by a few thieves.

### **Option C — Smuggler-Controlled Pier**

Held by the rival cell, forcing negotiation or stealth infiltration.

### **Option D — A Local Who Doesn’t Know What They’re Holding**

Someone like:
* A widow who took coin to “keep a package safe”
* The Gannet’s Trove owner, who thinks it’s magical
* A dockhand who hid it out of fear

### **When the Party Recovers the Box**

Veylan fulfills his end…
…but wants the party aboard quickly before imperial attention rises.

### **If the Mutineers Are Stopped**

Veylan retains control of his ship.
The Pale Bitch leaves Blacktide quietly.

### **If the Mutineers Succeed**

Imperials begin tightening the noose:

* Ascendant scouts arrive
* Increased patrols
* Piers observed
* Leaving becomes time-sensitive and hostile


## Characterization:

### Veylan Mirth

1. **Meeting the Party (First Impressions)**
   *"Ah… Kael’ith, always punctual. And you lot? You’ve got the look of those who’d rather be anywhere but here. Lucky for you, I like punctual. Sit, speak—make it quick, the sea waits for no one."*

2. **Discussing the Lockbox / The Job**
   *"That box… it isn’t dangerous, not unless pried open without knowing its purpose. But it’s mine, and I intend to get it back. You want passage off this rock, you’ll find it and bring it to me. Nothing fancy, nothing heroic—just bring it."*

3. **Reacting to Mutiny / Crew Issues**
   *"Keldran… again? I swear the man has the subtlety of a cannonball. If he acts tonight, I swear on the Pale Bitch that he’ll regret it. You handle him, or I’ll handle the lot myself—and you don’t want that."*

4. **Encouraging the Party**
   *"Outsiders can be useful. Clever ones, even more so. Don’t disappoint me. The seas beyond Karthold aren’t waiting, and neither am I."*

---

### Boatswain Keldran

1. **Suspicious / Confrontational**
   *"You think I trust you? You think I trust anyone not born to the deck? One wrong move, and I’ll see the both of you swinging off the rigging faster than you can blink."*

2. **Rallying Crew**
   *"Listen up, lads and lasses! The captain’s soft heart’ll be our ruin if we don’t act. These outsiders… they’ll drag us into Imperial hands faster than you can say ‘Harrivale.’ We protect our own—or we’re dead."*

3. **Negotiating / Testing the Party**
   *"I’m not unreasonable… yet. Show me you’re not rats in the hold, and maybe I’ll spare your hides. Fail me, and you’ll wish the tides had swallowed you first."*

4. **When Plotting Mutiny**
   *"Tonight, while they’re busy playing heroes… we make our move. Quiet, sharp, and careful. No heroics, no mistakes. The lockbox will be ours—or at least, out of the captain’s hands."*
