# HALDRATH: ROOT OF THE FIRST CURSE

A high-stakes D&D 5e adventure for Level 3 characters.

## Module Overview
Haldrath is a site of living corruption where environmental hazards and progressive "Black Blood" infection provide as much danger as the monsters within.

### Global Mechanics & Hazards
* **Bramble Terrain:** Thick overgrowth is difficult terrain. Ending a turn within it requires a **DC 11 STR save** or the creature is **Restrained** until the start of its next turn.
* **Black Vein Reaction:** When a glass-like vein is damaged, all creatures within 10 ft must succeed on a **DC 12 CON save** or take **1d6 necrotic damage**.
* **Smoke & Air:** In upper areas, every 10 minutes requires a **DC 10 CON save** or the character has disadvantage on their next Perception check.

---

## AREA 0: Chapel of the First Light (Safe Point)
A fractured ruin of layered human and Nimyrian architecture where melted stone walls drip like wax and the altar is split.

* **Mechanics:** This area temporarily suppresses corruption and grants advantage on the next save against fear or charm.
* **Key Event (The Lost Signal):** A divine interruption scene that provides the party with a **Long Rest** and stabilizes Zeraph's lantern.
* **Fixed Loot:** 2 Potions of Healing, 1 Holy Water, fine vestments (75 gp).
* **Variable Loot (1d6):**
    * (1–3) Incense and prayer relics (25 gp)
    * (4–5) Spell Scroll: *Protection from Evil and Good*
    * (6) **Necklace of Prayer Beads** (1 Bless bead)

---

## AREA 1: The Sunken Court (Transition Hub)
A warped basin where the ground slopes unnaturally toward a half-sunken keep.

* **Combat:** **2 Needle Blights** and **1 weakened Vine Blight** emerge as players approach the keep.
* **Mechanics:** Moving uphill is difficult terrain; moving downhill adds +5 ft movement. **Sprinting (Dash) requires a DC 10 DEX save or the character falls prone.**
* **Fixed Loot:** 40 gp, Potion of Climbing.
* **Variable Loot (1d6):**
    * (1–3) 1d6 x 5 gp, torch bundle, or healer's kit
    * (4–5) Potion of Heroism or Spell Scroll: *Guiding Bolt*
    * (6) **Driftglobe**

---

## AREA 2: The Keep Gate (Breach Point)
A tilted entrance cracked open but sealed by pressure-hardened bramble.

* **Combat (Optional):** **1 Vine Blight** emerges if the party is noisy.
* **The Breach (Puzzle/Pressure):** Players can force the gate (Athletics), burn bramble (triggers a spore hazard/CON save), or solve the Relief Puzzle. **Each failure causes brambles to lash out (+4 to hit, 1d6 piercing).**
* **Fixed Loot:** Climber’s Kit, 25 gp.
* **Variable Loot (1d6):**
    * (1–3) Rope, pitons, or rations
    * (4–5) Potion of Spider Climb or Spell Scroll: *Feather Fall*
    * (6) **Boots of Elvenkind**

---

## AREA 3: Keep Interior (Entry Hall)
A hall of muffled sound and sagging metal fixtures, notably devoid of any bodies.

* **Mechanics:** Sound dampening causes disadvantage on hearing-based Perception checks.
* **Hidden Hazard:** Disturbed debris requires a **DC 11 DEX save** or deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage and knocks the target prone.
* **Fixed Loot:** 30 gp, 1 Antitoxin.
* **Variable Loot (1d6):**
    * (1–3) 1d6 x 10 gp, signal whistle, or fine clothes
    * (4–5) Potion of Comprehension or Spell Scroll: *Detect Magic*
    * (6) **Sending Stone** (single)

---

## AREA 4: The War Room (Trigger Zone)
A command center filled with frantic notes and a warped map table.

* **⚠️ CORE MECHANIC (The Collapse):** When players begin searching or reading, the DM rolls **1d4+2 rounds**.
    * **Round 1:** DC 10 DEX save or stumble.
    * **Round 2:** DC 11 DEX save or fall prone.
    * **Round 3+:** DC 12 DEX save or fall prone and slide 5 ft toward the center.
* **Resolution:** On the final round, the floor gives way. **Players fall into Area 5, taking 2d6 falling damage (DC 12 DEX save for half).**
* **Fixed Loot:** 80 gp, Map Case.
* **Variable Loot (1d6):**
    * (1–3) Ink/parchment or navigation tools
    * (4–5) Spell Scroll: *Locate Object* or Potion of Resistance (necrotic)
    * (6) **+1 Weapon**

---

## AREA 5: The Thornshaft (Impact Zone)
A vertical wound in the earth filled with black ichor residue.

* **Mechanics:** Players land in a root mass and are Restrained (DC 11 STR/DEX save to free). **At Initiative 20, the roots shift, dealing 1d4 piercing damage to one restrained creature.**
* **Details:** Stone carvings repeat the warning **"STOP DIGGING"** dozens of times.
* **Fixed Loot:** 1 Potion of Healing, 15 gp.
* **Variable Loot (1d6):**
    * (1–3) Rope, rations, or waterskin
    * (4–5) Potion of Growth or Spell Scroll: *Longstrider*
    * (6) **Ring of Jumping**

---

## AREA 6: Vein Tunnels
Caverns fused with glass-like veins that pulse rhythmically with ichor.

* **Combat:** **1–2 Gray Oozes** (veinbound forms).
* **Mechanics (Living Ground):** The first time a creature moves each turn, they must succeed on a **DC 10 DEX save** or lose 5 ft of movement.
* **Tactical Note:** Veins can be targeted (AC 10, 5 HP). Destroying them triggers a pulse but opens paths.
* **Fixed Loot:** Alchemical supplies (50 gp), 1 vial of strange ichor.
* **Variable Loot (1d6):**
    * (1–3) Acid vial or Alchemist’s fire
    * (4–5) Potion of Resistance (necrotic) or Spell Scroll: *Ray of Sickness*
    * (6) **Wand of Magic Missiles** (1d4 charges)

---

## AREA 7: The First Pool
A perfectly reflective reservoir of black ichor.

* **Mental Hazard:** Starting a turn within 10 ft requires a **DC 11 WIS save** or the player has disadvantage on their next attack or ability check.
* **Temptation Interaction:** As a bonus action, a player can voluntarily gain advantage on their next attack in exchange for gaining corruption.
* **Fixed Loot:** 1 Potion of Greater Healing (hidden), set of empty flasks.
* **Variable Loot (1d6):**
    * (1–3) Herbalism kit or empty vials
    * (4–5) Potion of Heroism or Spell Scroll: *Blindness/Deafness*
    * (6) **Ring of Mind Shielding**

---

## AREA 8: The Rooted Gate
A living bramble seal blocking the deeper passage.

* **Combat:** **1 Vine Blight** (enhanced HP).
* **Mechanics:** Tight space grants disadvantage to ranged attacks beyond 20 ft. At Initiative 20, one target must make a **DC 12 STR save** or be restrained.
* **Fixed Loot:** Explorer’s Pack, 100 gp salvage.
* **Variable Loot (1d6):**
    * (1–3) 1d6 x 10 gp, antitoxin, or hunting trap
    * (4–5) Potion of Growth or Spell Scroll: *Enlarge/Reduce*
    * (6) **Staff of the Adder**

---

## AREA 9: The Heart Vein
The source of the curse: a massive pulsing waterfall of Black Blood.

* **Combat (Optional):** **1 Darkmantle** or **Shadow**.
* **Mechanics (Pulse Field):** Every round at Initiative 20, all creatures must make a **DC 12 CON save** or take 1d6 necrotic damage or gain disadvantage on their next roll.
* **Trigger Event:** Any major interaction causes the vein to open, destabilizing the area and beginning the escape.
* **Fixed Loot:** 150 gp in gems, 1 vial of Black Blood (Plot Item).
* **Variable Loot (1d6):**
    * (1–3) 2 Potions of Healing or 1d6 x 10 gp
    * (4–5) **+1 Weapon** (choice) or *Cloak of Protection*
    * (6) **Ring of Resistance (necrotic)**

---

## CLIMAX: Escape — The Black Flood
Triggered by interacting with the Heart Vein, causing the cavern to destabilize.

* **Skill Challenge:** The party needs **6 successes before 3 failures** to escape.
* **Combat Pressure:** **1 corrupted beast appears every 2 rounds (2 spawn if the party engages).**
* **Hazards:**
    * **Rising Ichor:** Round 2+ (difficult terrain); Round 4+ (DC 12 STR save or speed halved).
    * **Bramble Surge:** Initiative 20 (DC 12 STR or restrained).
    * **Falling Debris:** DC 12 DEX save or **1d6 bludgeoning**.
* **Greed Mechanic:** Each piece of optional loot (satchels, fragments) grabbed mid-escape counts as **1 automatic failure** for the skill challenge.
