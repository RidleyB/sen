Sometime in the quiet hours, Riot wakes with a sharp breath.

For a moment, he is not in Grave’s End. Not in Corrin’s cellar. Not free.

His hand moves beneath the blanket, searching blindly for his leg.

It finds empty space.

Riot goes still.

He does not curse. He does not shout. He only stares down at the shape the blanket fails to make, his jaw tight, his breathing shallow.

Across one fist, the tattoo reads **Riot**.

Across the other, half-hidden in the dark, **Ruin**.

Kael’ith is awake across the room. Of course he is. He sees it happen, and for once has no plan, no careful words, no way to make himself useful.

Riot notices him watching.

For a long moment, neither of them speaks.

Then Riot pulls the blanket back over the missing place and looks away.

“Don’t,” he says quietly.

Kael’ith lowers his eyes.

Riot’s voice is smaller when he speaks again.

“I keep forgetting.”

The room settles around the words.

After a while, Kael’ith says, “I’m sorry.”

Riot almost laughs, but there is no strength in it.

“Which part?”

Kael’ith has no answer.

Riot closes his tattooed hands and turns toward the wall.

“I don’t know how to go back like this.”

No one in the room tells him he can.

No one lies quickly enough.
