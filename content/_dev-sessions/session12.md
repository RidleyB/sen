## **⚠️ COLLAPSE MECHANIC**
### **Trigger**
* When players begin searching, reading, or spending time here

### **Start Timer**
Roll: **1d4 + 2 rounds**
Do NOT tell players the number.

### **Escalation Each Round**
**Round 1:**
* Subtle shifting underfoot
* Dust falls from ceiling
* Faint cracking sound

**Round 2:**
* Map table tilts slightly
* Stone splits form in floor
* Low rumble begins

**Round 3+:**
* Sections of floor dip
* Objects slide toward center
* Audible cracking, louder each round

### **Final Round: COLLAPSE**
* Floor gives way completely
* Players fall into the **Thornshaft**

### **Fall Resolution**
* 2d6 falling damage (Dex save DC 12 for half)
* Land in tangled root/briar mass below

👉 Important:
They don’t choose to descend.

## BLACK BLOOD

### **Black Blood Corruption System (Imar’s Ichor)**

This is a **progressive temptation + degradation track**. Early stages give insight or power. Mid stages start taxing the character. High stages become dangerous and disruptive.

### **Trigger**
Any time a character:
* Fails a Wisdom save against Black Blood influence
* Drinks or directly channels the ichor
* Spends prolonged time in heavy exposure zones

They **roll on the Black Blood Table**.

### **ROLL PROGRESSION**
Each time they roll, they use a larger die:

| Exposure Count | Die |
| -------------- | --- |
| 1st            | d4  |
| 2nd            | d6  |
| 3rd            | d8  |
| 4th            | d10 |
| 5th            | d12 |
| 6th+           | d20 |

👉 Always keep the **highest result rolled** as their current corruption tier.

### **BLACK BLOOD EFFECT TABLE (1–20)**

#### **1–4: The Whispering Edge (Safe / Tempting)**

**1 — Faint Echo**
You hear distant whispers.
Advantage on one Perception check (DM chooses when).

**2 — Lingering Vision**
You glimpse something true but incomplete.
Gain advantage on one Investigation check.

**3 — Pulse Alignment**
Your body syncs briefly with the environment.
+1 to initiative for the next combat.

**4 — Borrowed Instinct**
You react faster than thought.
Once before next rest: add +1d4 to any roll.

#### **5–8: The First Rot (Noticeable Cost)**

**5 — Uneasy Breath**
Disadvantage on next Con save.

**6 — Blood Heat**
Take 1d4 necrotic damage immediately.
Gain advantage on next attack roll.

**7 — Shadow Hunger**
Disadvantage on first Wisdom save each day.

**8 — Thorned Flesh**
When hit by melee attack: take +1 damage (skin splitting subtly)

#### **9–12: The Deeping Curse (Mechanical Pressure)**

**9 — Warped Reflex**
-1 AC (body moving unpredictably)

**10 — Echoing Mind**
Disadvantage on Concentration checks

**11 — Beast Pulse**
When bloodied (half HP), must succeed DC 11 Wis save or:
* Move toward nearest creature and attack (1 turn)

**12 — Black Veins**
Max HP reduced by 3 (restored when corruption lowered)

#### **13–16: The Breaking Self (Loss of Control)**

**13 — Twisted Form**
Speed reduced by 5 ft
Advantage on Intimidation checks

**14 — Hunger Rising**
Must succeed DC 12 Wis save when seeing blood or:
* Lose action (overcome by urge)

**15 — Vein Flare**
When taking damage:
* Take +1d4 necrotic damage

**16 — Fractured Will**
Disadvantage on all Wisdom saving throws

#### **17–19: The Near Turn (Severe Corruption)**

**17 — Half-Form**
Physical mutation visible
* Disadvantage on Persuasion
* Advantage on Strength checks

**18 — Predatory Surge**
Once per combat:
* Must use action to attack nearest creature (no save)

**19 — Fading Self**
At start of turn:
* DC 13 Wis save or lose bonus action + reaction

#### **20: Imar’s Claim (Critical State)**
You are not dead—but you are **no longer stable**.

Effects:
* Disadvantage on all saving throws
* At start of each turn:
  * DC 14 Wis save or:
    * Lose control (DM controls character for 1 turn as violent, primal version)

* You **cannot benefit from Inspiration or advantage from allies**

👉 This is the point where:
**They must seek help, or they will become an NPC soon.**

### **LOWERING CORRUPTION (IMPORTANT)**
This must be **possible, but not trivial**.

#### **PASSIVE REDUCTION**

**Long Rest in Safe Conditions**
* Reduce corruption by **1 tier**
* Requires:
  * No exposure
  * Clean water / stable environment

### **ACTIVE METHODS**

#### **1. Purging Rest (Ritual)**
* 1 hour
* DC 12 Con save
* Success: reduce by **2 tiers**
* Failure: take 1d6 damage, reduce by 1 tier

Requires:
* Clean fire
* Herbalism kit or healer’s kit

#### **2. Divine / Radiant Aid**
* Spells like:
  * *Lesser Restoration*: reduce by 1 tier
  * *Protection from Evil and Good*: suppress effects for 1 hour
* Stronger magic (future access) removes more

#### **3. Abstinence**
* If a character avoids ALL exposure for a full session/day:
  * Reduce by 1 additional tier

#### **4. Dangerous Purge (Player Choice)**
Drink alcohol / poison / purge mixture
* Con save DC 13:
  * Success: reduce 2 tiers
  * Failure:
    * Gain 1 exhaustion
    * Reduce 1 tier
