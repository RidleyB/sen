# **1. Dockside Arrival (Opening Scene)**

### **Atmosphere**

* Ash falling like snow
* Dockworkers shouting through cloth masks
* Refugees arriving from inland

### **Key Beats**

* Dead sailors carried off
* Captain Veylan:

  * pays / acknowledges the party
  * quietly separates (moving the lockbox if noticed)
* Crew shaken → reinforces stakes

### **Hooks Dropped**

* “Fires are spreading faster than they should”
* “Druids lost control”
* “Something’s wrong in the forest”

---

# **2. Player Choice Hub (Let Them Lead)**

Players will naturally choose one path:

### **A. Follow Kael’ith & Riot**

→ Leads to **Coren + rebellion context**

### **B. Explore Grave’s End**

→ Leads to **market, rumors, tone building**

### **C. Ask About Fires**

→ Leads to **Ashborn problem directly**

**Important:**
All paths converge on the same conclusion:

> The fires are the immediate problem.

---

# **3. Hollow Vein Quarter (Mandatory Pass-Through)**

### **Scene**

* Emaciated figures, trembling hands
* Cloth-wrapped faces, coughing fits
* Blackened veins more pronounced

### **Interactions**

* Medicine DC 12 → stabilize someone
* Insight/Arcana:

  * disease worsened by smoke
* NPC whispers:

  > “The fire feeds it…”
  > “It spreads faster now…”

### **Purpose**

* Stakes become personal
* Connect:

  * fire
  * Wound
  * suffering

---

# **4. Coren Velrik (If Followed or Found)**

### **Info Delivered**

* Rebellion fractured since Riot’s capture
* Emergence of **a more aggressive faction (Prophet hinted)**
* Imperial retaliation escalating

### **Key Line**

> “Every strike we make now, they answer somewhere else.”

---

## **Kael’ith & Riot Plan (Post-Coren)**

### **Initial Goal**

* Reconnect with outer rebellion cells
* Rebuild fractured network

### **Attempt**

* Plan to leave Grave’s End via smuggler routes

### **Immediate Problem**

* Fires have made travel impossible:

  * routes burned
  * visibility gone
  * wildlife and druids destabilized

---

## **Conclusion**

They pivot:

> “We fix this first—or nothing else matters.”

### **New Plan**

* Stabilize Grave’s End
* Contain the fire spread
* Investigate Ashborn activity

# **5. Market / Information Gathering (Optional)**

  * “The burn is intentional”

### **Mechanical Notes**

* Increased prices (food, potions, supplies)
* Smoke gear available

---

# **6. Outskirts Transition**

### **Leaving the City**

* Refugees entering as party exits
* Guards warn:

  > “You won’t find a path out there.”

---

## **Short Travel Skill Challenge**

**3 successes before 2 failures**

### **Skills**

* Survival / Nature → navigate
* Perception → avoid flare-ups
* Athletics → traverse debris

### **Failure Effects**

* 1d4 fire damage
* disorientation
* slower progress

---

# **7. Beast Rampage Encounter (Midway Event)**

## **Setup**

* Smoke thickens
* Ground shakes
* A corrupted beast crashes through fleeing wildlife

## **Skill Challenge**

**4 successes before 3 failures**

### **Goals**

* Avoid the beast
* Escape fire corridor

### **Failure Outcome**

* Partial combat + worse positioning

### **Success Outcome**

* Clean escape, minimal damage

### **Key Detail**

* Beast behaves unnaturally
* May hesitate toward direction of ruins

---

# **8. Fire Zone Escalation**

### **Environment**

* Burning canopy
* collapsing trees
* blackened root systems visible

### **Foreshadowing**

* Roots seep black ichor
* Fire behaves inconsistently

---

# **9. Ashborn Druid Encounter**

## **Scene**

* Mixed druid group at fireline

### **Composition**

* 1 Ashen-Touched Druid (corrupted influence)
* 2 Wardens (martial)
* 1 Tender (non-corrupted support)

---

## **Interaction Options**

### **Social**

* Learn:

  * fire is holding something back
  * control is slipping
  * something beneath the ruins is spreading

### **Insight**

* One druid is pushing escalation:

  > “Let it burn.”

### **Combat**

* Multi-role fight:

  * frontliners + control + hazards

---

## **Key Reveal**

> “The fire was meant to contain it… now it’s the only thing slowing it.”
