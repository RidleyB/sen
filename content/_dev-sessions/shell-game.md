## Side Quest — *Shell Game*

**Party Level:** 2
**Time to Run:** 30–60 minutes
**Purpose:**

* Ground the party in Blacktide
* Build goodwill on the docks
* Offer low-risk resources without gold bloat

## Lead-In — How the Party Gets Pulled In

As the party moves along the pier—either heading toward **The Scuttle’s Lantern** or away from **Veylan’s warehouse**—they pass a stilt-house half over the water.

A woman is struggling with a **crate of live crabs**, one arm braced against the railing, the other shoving the lid closed as claws scrape from inside. She swears under her breath.

If the party slows or watches:

> “Don’t just stand there—either help or keep walkin’!”

Nearby dockhands:

* Laugh, but don’t offer help
* One mutters: “Ain’t goin’ under *that* house.”

If the party assists with the crate or asks questions, she introduces herself.

## NPC — Marra Teln

**Marra Teln**
Human dockworker’s widow, mid-40s

* Sleeves rolled, clothes reeking of brine and smoke
* Blunt, tired, not inclined toward heroics
* Knows most people on the pier by name

Her house:
* Stilted, half over the water
* Storage space beneath the floorboards: nets, crab pots, a tied skiff

### The Ask (Natural Dialogue)

Once she realizes the party isn’t just gawking:
> “They came up with the tide. Big ones. Mean ones.
> I can hear ’em scraping at night.
> I just want my house back.”

She explains:
* Something is living under the house
* It’s blocking access to her gear
* It’s getting worse, not better

### Offer

She can’t pay much, but she’s serious.
* **A small pouch of silver** (enough to matter, not enough to haggle hard over)
* **30 rations of dried crab meat** (salted, smoky, dock staple)

If pressed:

> “That’s what I’ve got. Take it or don’t.”

## The Situation (DM Truth)

This is not magical.

* A barrel of **fish oil and offal** cracked during the last storm
* The scent drew crabs in large numbers
* Confined space made them territorial and aggressive

The crabs now block:

* Crab pots and nets
* A skiff tied beneath the house
* Structural supports holding up part of the floor

Left alone long enough, the house *will* suffer damage.

## Location — Under-House Crawlspace

### Physical Layout

* Clearance: **4–5 feet** in places
* Rotting planks, hanging kelp, rope tangles
* Water depth:
  * **Ankle-deep** at low tide
  * **Waist-deep** at high tide
* Visibility is poor without light

### Environmental Hazards
**Slippery Footing**
* Moving quickly or fighting recklessly:
  * **Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 10**
  * Failure: prone or disadvantage on next attack

**Weakened Support Beam**
* Heavy impacts or fire damage risk collapse
* If stressed:
  * Partial collapse (difficult terrain, 1d4 bludgeoning damage to creatures beneath)

## Creatures — Giant Crabs

Use **Giant Crab** (MM p. 324)
### Adjustments (Narrative, Not Mechanical Overhaul)

* Shells scarred by hooks and nets
* Hyper-territorial behavior
* One noticeably larger “alpha” crab near the leaking barrel

**Optional Tweaks**
* +1 AC (barnacle-thick shells)
* Alpha crab: +5 HP

Crabs:
* Fight aggressively
* Do **not** pursue beyond the crawlspace

## Approaches
### 1. Direct Clearing
* Tight, awkward combat
* Noisy
* Risk of structural damage
* Draws attention from dockhands above

### 2. Lure & Trap
* Use fish, oil, or rations as bait
* Tip crab pots or nets to restrain
* Slower, safer, cleaner

### 3. Environmental Fix
* Locate and remove or seal the leaking barrel
* Intentionally collapse part of the crawlspace to deny access
* Requires care to avoid damaging the house

### Failure / Recklessness Risks
* Broken support beam
* Soaked or ruined supplies
* Reduced silver reward
* Marra’s goodwill drops sharply

## Complications (Optional, One at Most)

* The tide starts coming in mid-encounter
* A dock rival heckles or places bets

## Resolution
Once resolved:
Marra inspects the damage personally.

* If successful:
  * Pays as promised
  * **Silver + dried crab rations**
* If the house is mostly intact:
  * Warm meal
  * Favorable word spread along the docks

> “You’re not useless. That counts for something here.”

## World Impact
* Dock residents recognize the party
* The party gains a reputation for handling *unpleasant local problems*
* Small goodwill buffer in Blacktide:
  * Easier conversations
  * Fewer closed doors
  * A reason people talk to them instead of about them

This side quest reinforces that **survival in Blacktide is about earning trust one small problem at a time**, not heroics.
