---
title: Dawnspire
header:
   excerpt: "*“Construct for me a tower worthy of shedding my light.”*"
---

A colossal citadel of white stone and solid light, rising like a blade into the heavens. It is said to pierce the veil between the mortal realm and Nimyr’s radiant domain. From its highest platform, the Eternal Argent Dawn projects light across the entire world. Few mortals ever see the inner halls, where it’s rumored that living angels of silver flame stand guard.
