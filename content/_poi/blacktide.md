Blacktide is a dwindling coastal settlement on the northwest edge of Karthold. Its residents mostly survive through smuggling, trade, and occasional export of ore, stone, and fish to Palor in the Outer Provinces. The town features a few taverns and shops for travelers, adventurers, and sailors.

## Overview

{% include figure popup=true image_path="/assets/images/blacktide.png" %}{: .align-right}

**Population:** ~350 permanent residents, swelling to ~500–600 when smugglers, miners, or passing sailors are in port.

**Demographics:** Predominantly tiefling

**Primary Income Sources:**
* **Ore export** (declining): iron, copper, and low-grade silver from the inner-island mines
* **Dock fees and repairs:** small shipyard, ropewalk, and patchwork hull repairs
* **Smuggling:** extremely common, unofficially the lifeblood of the settlement
* **Fishing:** enough to keep the town fed, barely profitable
* **Taverns:** sailors and smugglers spend freely while ashore

**Economic Health:** Poor. Blacktide is in slow decay. Half the warehouses near the piers sit abandoned, and only one of the old ore-processing mills still runs. Streets sag with disrepair, and wood rot is common. The port still functions, but it’s running on momentum rather than growth.

Blacktide is built into two main levels, shaped by the island’s natural cliff terraces and constant coastal erosion.

### Disposition

Blacktide’s population shares a rough, sea-bitten resilience. Generations have lived in salt, wind, and economic decay, and it’s shaped a culture that is:

* Closed off to outsiders
* Mutually protective of locals
* Highly pragmatic, valuing deals, reputation, and loyalty over laws

They are not openly hostile unless provoked, but trust comes slowly. Every outsider who arrives is quietly watched from doorways, alleys, and the pier railings. Blacktiders assume any newcomer wants something.

| **Group**                                                     | **Blacktide’s Attitude**                    | **Notes**                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Outsiders / Travelers**                                     | Wary, closed-off, transactional.            | Outsiders are watched closely; trust is earned slowly.                   |
| **Smugglers**                                                 | Cautiously friendly; economically welcomed. | Treated as the lifeblood of the town. Given priority service.   |
| **Imperials / Ascendants**                                    | Quiet hostility, deep suspicion.            | Blamed for Blacktide’s decline. Locals tighten up, avoid conversation, and keep a low profile around imperial agents. |
| **Local Tiefling Residents**                                  | Protective, communal, mutually loyal.       | Internal disagreements exist, but the town closes ranks against any external threat or pressure.                      |


## Inns & Taverns

{% include figure popup=true image_path="/assets/images/old-keelhouse.png" %}{: .align-right}
### **The Old Keelhouse**

*Warm, dim, and weathered. The interior smells of tea, lingering spice, and simmering stew. Wooden beams arch overhead like an inverted boat keel, giving the entire common room a maritime hush. Lamps are shielded with old sailcloth to soften the light. Patrons speak quietly sharing stories, while Tollin moves slowly between tables with the calm of a seasoned sailor long retired.*

| Service               | Description                                                         | Price        |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Room (shared dorm)    | Basic room with straw mattresses, shared space (4 max)              | 2 sp / night |
| Room (private)        | Small private room with straw mattress, modest furnishings (2 max)  | 5 sp / night |
| Meal                  | Hardtack, salted fish, tea or watered-down ale                      | 3 sp         |
| Ale / Grog (per pint) | Local brew, low-quality but filling                                 | 1 sp         |
| Specialty Sea Grog    | Stronger rum-based mix, sailors’ favorite                           | 2 sp         |

**Owner:** **Tollin Var**, a retired Tiefling navigator with pale gray skin, short curved horns, and golden eyes. Missing two fingers on his left hand, he leans on a cane when moving through the inn.

**Location:** Upper ridge of Blacktide

**Notes:**
* The Old Keelhouse is quieter and more respectable than The Scuttle’s Lantern, attracting older sailors or merchants who prefer conversation and a safe place to rest.
* Rooms are small and sparse but provide basic comfort; meals are hearty, simple, and designed for travelers used to rough living.
* Tollin may offer information about Blacktide, nearby smuggling routes, or the harbor if persuaded or paid.

### **The Scuttle’s Lantern**

*Loud, sweaty, and soaked in the scent of rum and brine. Lanterns swing overhead, rattling with every footfall or slammed mug. Sea shanties, arguments, and laughter blend into a chaotic chorus. The walls are plastered with harpoons, dented helmets, and trophies from drunken dares. The whole place vibrates with the restless energy of sailors fresh off a hard run—or looking to start trouble before their next one.*

| **“Wrecker’s Brew”**              | Siren’s specialty fermented concoction; black as pitch                  | 3 sp         |

**Owner:** **Siren**, a Tiefling with seafoam-green skin, long swept-back horns, and a jawline tattooed in white ink to resemble lantern light. She’s boisterous, loud, and keeps a naval boarding axe under the counter—not for show. She treats regulars like family and outsiders like entertainment until they earn her respect.

**Location:** Dockside, one street up from the seawall. 

**Notes:**
* Siren knows every smuggler crew by name and reputation; she won't gossip to outsiders, however.
* Her inn is where the mutinous members of the smuggler crew often drink—eavesdropping or tailing attempts begin here.
* The Captain (who Kael’ith knows) of the Cold Bitch avoids The Scuttle’s Lantern specifically because his crew members start trouble here.
* A brawl can break out at any moment if the party pushes the wrong sailor or mentions the prison too loudly.
