The Oubliette is a fortress-prison carved directly into the bedrock beneath Karthold, hanging off the rocky southwest coastline. It functions as both a penitentiary and a monument to despair, its spiraling cells descending toward the earth’s core. Prisoners rarely survive, though the distant echoes of screams suggest the Empire finds continued use for those who remain alive.

It also serves as the administrative and operational heart of Karthold’s governance, housing offices and guard posts for the governor and their Ascendant overseers. The prison’s architecture reflects both authority and menace, with walls and corridors designed as much to intimidate as to contain.

## Known Sections

### Upper Levels

* **Condition:** Mostly intact, signs of order and administration remain
* **Notable Features:** Guard posts, supply corridors, offices
* **Threats:** Standard prison hazards, environmental decay

### Abandoned Sections: Lower Levels

* **Condition:** Overrun by corruption and neglect
* **Notable Features:** Twisting corridors, derelict cells, failed fortifications
* **Hazards:**
  * **Blight:** Rapidly spreading briarthorn, twisted vegetation thriving in damp corridors
  * **Beastmen Curse:** Humanoid creatures warped by ancient curses, hostile to intruders

### Sluice Runoff

* **Condition:** Narrow, damp channels beneath the prison carrying waste and alchemical runoff. Damaged.
* **Notable Features:** Sickly green glow from chemical contamination, unstable stone, claustrophobic tunnels
* **Hazards:** Corrosive waters, lurking predators, treacherous footing
