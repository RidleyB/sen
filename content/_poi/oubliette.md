---
title: Oubliette
---

A fortress-prison carved into the stone at the heart of Karthold, acting as the central hub to its inner workings. The Oubliette is both a penitentiary and a monument to despair — its cells descend in endless spirals below ground, rumored to reach into the warmer core of the earth itself. Prisoners who enter rarely leave alive, though the screams echoing through the lower halls suggest the Empire has found uses for those who do. The Oubliette serves as the command center of Karthold’s governor and its Ascendant overseers.
