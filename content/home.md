## THE WORLD OF MORDT

Before **Nimyr**, the world was darkness and dust.

The lands of Mordt were shrouded in endless night — a silent wasteland where only the faint glow of dying stars touched the horizon. Humanity lived in scattered tribes, huddled around the ruins of forgotten gods. The old powers were fading, and the world itself seemed to wither alongside them.

Then came Nimyr, the Lightbringer. His arrival split the sky in two, and for the first time, the people of Mordt saw a dawn that did not die. He offered them warmth, order, and purpose. In the centuries that followed, his chosen — the Ascendants — rose as living vessels of his divine will, shaping the Nimyrian Empire from the ashes of the old world.

Now, all corners of civilization bask in the cold, silver glow of the Dawnspire, the towering monument that channels Nimyr’s light across the realm. Its radiance never fades. Its shadow never moves. To most, Nimyr is salvation made flesh.

**To others, he is the jailer of the world.**

## THE EDGE OF THE EMPIRE - KARTHOLD

North of the mainland, where the light grows dim lies Karthold- the empire’s edge, and its grave. To Ascendants, it is a thriving mining colony rich with silver and salt. To everyone else, it has become a prison island where the condemned are sent to vanish. Criminals, debtors, rebels, and the faithless all meet the same fate: chains, cold stone, and endless labor beneath the glare of a sun that never rises nor sets.

At the heart of the island looms the Oubliette, the underground fortress-prison that oversees the mines. Its walls are said to be fused with living silver — a gift from the Ascendants themselves — and its Warden, Malrec, is one of their kind. Here, the Ascendants’ word is law, and their light is as cruel as it is divine.		

**It is within these depths that you find yourself**. You bear your own reasons for being here — wrongfully imprisoned, a rebel in hiding, or a criminal in truth — yet all share the same truth: in Karthold, no one leaves alive.

## RISE FROM THE ASHES

Yet whispers stir in the dark.

Of a flame of rebellion being kindled; some say Nimyr’s radiance is eternal, but others say even the brightest flame must one day burn out.

And somewhere, in the silence between the stars, a spark is waiting — a spark that may one day ignite the end of Nimyr’s argent dawn.

Your story begins where all revolutions do: **in chains.**
