Vampires are a predatory, sentient species known for their longevity, nocturnal habits, and need to feed on the blood of living beings. Historically, their activity has been concentrated in the outer provinces, though reports occasionally emerge from the inner provinces. Their presence is often difficult to track, as they can move individually or operate within covens, coordinating attacks and maintaining secrecy.

Remote villages are frequent targets of vampire predation, with reports of entire settlements being drained or, in some cases, fully converted into vampire communities. These transformations are generally shrouded in rumor, but survivors’ accounts describe villages abandoned by day and filled with unnatural activity after dusk. Vampires are noted for their cunning, often integrating into local society under disguise before revealing their true nature.

While few organized responses exist in the inner provinces, the outer provinces have seen localized militias and hunters emerge to counter vampire activity. Despite these efforts, the species’ adaptability and secrecy make them a persistent and unpredictable threat. Their impact is both direct, through predation, and indirect, as fear spreads through affected regions, disrupting trade, travel, and governance.
