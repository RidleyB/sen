title: "Thralldom"
header:

Thralldom is a long-standing institution in Nimyria in which Ascendants claim individual Humans or Tieflings as personal thralls. While humans are the most common, tieflings may also be taken more rarely. Thralls are fully subject to the authority of their masters and are generally forbidden from speaking to or interacting with anyone outside their designated Ascendant. They exist primarily to serve, obey, and entertain, with their lives tightly controlled through ritual, intimidation, and magical oversight.

Thralls fulfill a variety of roles. They may tend to necropolises, perform ritual work on behalf of their masters, or act as participants in sanctioned games of combat for the amusement of the Ascendant elite. Some are used for sadistic or fringe indulgences, including sexual exploitation, as part of the personal entertainments of their owners. Others are trained in specialized labor or magical tasks to further the Ascendant’s influence and power.

The acquisition of thralls remains a formalized practice in the inner provinces, where Ascendants may purchase or select new subjects from designated markets or private estates. These venues cater specifically to the elite, offering thralls with particular attributes, skills, or appearance to match the desires of the buyer. Thralls are considered both tools and symbols of status, reflecting the reach and taste of the Ascendant who controls them.

Cultural perception of Thralldom varies widely. Among the citizenry of the inner provinces, it is generally seen as a normative, if morally unsettling, aspect of elite life. In the Outer Provinces, thralls are viewed as a stark reminder of Ascendant domination, inspiring fear, resentment, and occasional rebellion. Despite these tensions, the practice continues largely unchecked, maintained by tradition, wealth, and the absolute authority of the Ascendant class.
