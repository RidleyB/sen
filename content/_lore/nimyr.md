Nimyr is the godlike figure at the heart of the Nimyrian Empire, a celestial being whose light shapes the world of Mordt. History says that before Nimyr came, Mordt was a land of darkness and dust, where humans struggled to survive under a dying sky. When he appeared, his light split the heavens and touched the earth, offering warmth, order, and power. Those who accepted his gift became the first Nimyrians. Humans transformed, marked by horns, tails, and the silver fire of his blessing.

To most, Nimyr is a savior, a luminous god who drives away the darkness and maintains life across the empire. His **Dawnspire**, a colossal tower of radiant silver, channels his presence and spreads light far across the empire. Its glow allows fertile lands to flourish near the inner provinces, and its brilliance enforces the Ascendants’ rule. Many mortals live and work under Nimyr’s gaze without question, believing his light to be divine mercy.

Yet whispers among the oppressed speak a darker truth. Nimyr’s light is not purely benevolent. Those who serve him too zealously become Ascendants-immortal, powerful, and cruel- while others are bound to labor and servitude to maintain his empire. Some Nimyrians have even come to fear that the Dawnspire’s brilliance is drawn from the lives and souls of mortals themselves, a radiant dominion built on suffering.

Though no mortal fully understands Nimyr, it is clear his presence dominates every corner of Mordt. Rebellion is whispered as treasonous, and hope is measured in flickers beneath the argent dawn.
