The Ashborn Druids are a secluded druidic circle based in the shadowed outer provinces of Mordt, devoted to the cleansing and renewal of the land through fire. They view flame not merely as destruction, but as a purifying force, capable of burning away corruption, disease, and stagnation so that new life may flourish. Their rituals are quiet and solemn, centered around ash-marked circles, carefully tended embers, and smoke rising in meditative prayer.

Members of the Ashborn maintain a deep attunement to the suffering of the world, known among them as the Wound: a lingering corruption at the heart of the earth that festers beneath the surface. While most see fire as a tool or weapon, the Ashborn listen to its voice, using it to respond to the land’s pain rather than as an instrument of conquest. They are wary of external powers, including the silver light of Nimyr, which they believe suppresses primal spirits and binds the natural world beneath artificial order.

**The Circle enforces a strict code known as the Tenets of Ash:**

>- **Respect the Flame:** Fire is sacred; it must never be used for petty revenge or senseless destruction.
>- **Do Not Harm the Innocent:** The weak, wounded, and unwilling are untouchable.
>- **Heed the World’s Pain:** Ignoring the land’s suffering is a grave failing.
>- **In Death, Life:** Every act of destruction must be balanced with restoration or penance.
>- **Defend the Circle:** Secrets of the Ashborn must never be revealed; betrayal brings exile.
>- **Walk in Truth:** Deception and selfish ambition are forbidden.

The Ashborn are few in number and little-known beyond their provinces, earning a reputation as quiet, enigmatic guardians of the wounded earth. They are respected—and feared—by those who witness their pyres and rites, as their flames promise both destruction and renewal.
