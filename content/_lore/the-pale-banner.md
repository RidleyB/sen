The Pale Banner is a small criminal group moving in the shadows of the Outer Provinces. Most people have only heard of them in whispers—some say they are nothing more than a gang of petty thieves, others claim they have bigger ambitions, though no one has seen proof. Street gossip hints that they handle thefts, smuggling, and occasional spying, but these stories are often contradictory. Some claim the Pale Banner can be helpful if approached carefully, while others warn that crossing them will bring trouble.

They are not feared like any larger gangs or mercenary companies, but their presence in the city is noted by those who deal in the shadows. Most merchants and commoners have never met them and would not even recognize their symbol, an simple off-white or canvas banner.
