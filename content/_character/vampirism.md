Long before Nimyr’s argent dawn illuminated Mordt, before even the first human civilizations took form, there was Thagar the Hungering — a dark, godlike being of famine, shadow, and thirst. Much is unknown about this figure, besides an eternal hunger that all those cursed by his blood come to know.

When Nimyr’s light spread across the world, it burned away much of the old darkness- but not Thagar’s. His power sank deeper, sleeping beneath Mordt’s cold crust until a few centuries ago, when something began to stir once more. The curse of Vampirism began to creep though Nimyria. A sickness of blood and memory that awakens in those whose souls still resonate with the world before the Dawn.

The cursed live in shadow, hunted by the Empire for their resistance to Nimyr’s light and feared by mortals for what they might become. Some have found strength in unity; scattered covens, hidden brotherhoods, and blood cults bound by the shared hunger. Yet even these are small and secretive, for the Ascendants offer no mercy to the children of Thagar.

All vampires, however distant their lineage or controlled their thirst, share one haunting truth: the call of Sunder. Along the southern shore of Mordt, when the horizon is clear, the Isle of Sunder can be glimpsed — a mist-shrouded isle to the south, said to be where Thagar rests, and where his power still stirs. Every vampire feels it; a pull across blood and sea, whispering of a home they’ve never known.
