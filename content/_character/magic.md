Arcane magic is everywhere and nowhere in Mordt — **pervasive, but restricted**. The Ascendants wield it freely, claiming only they have been “perfected” enough to bear the Dawn’s flame. Mortals who practice it without sanction are heretics, rebels, or smugglers of forbidden knowledge.

* **Wizards** are secret archivists, hiding spellbooks in false walls or stolen vaults.
* **Sorcerers** are often born under strange circumstances. Curses, enchantments, or other strange happenings surrounding a childbirth leads to innate magic in the baby.
* **Bards** travel between settlements, weaving truths as songs to disguise forbidden lore.

> *In play:* Arcane casters must balance secrecy and ambition. The Empire watches closely, and Ascendants often seek to **recruit, capture, or “purify”** them for unauthorized power.
