title: Druidism
header:
   excerpt: "*“Heretics shall burn in the light of dawn.”*"
---

Druids are remnants of an ancient order that predates the rise of Nimyr’s light. They believe that Mordt once was alive, thriving, before something cursed the land with cold and darkness. The Empire calls them **“Ashborn”**; heretics who whisper to the dead soil and commune with spirits long thought extinct.

They operate in **secret circles**, hidden deep within ruins and cold forests, guarding the last seeds of wild life. Some see themselves as healers trying to **restore balance to a poisoned world**, while others see themselves as **agents of decay**, hastening the rot that will one day reclaim all.

> *In play:* Druids are rare, secretive, and often persecuted. Their power draws on Mordt’s wounded lifeforce rather than celestial blessing. They may feel a connection to the earth itself, sensing that some life still breathes in the cold earth.
