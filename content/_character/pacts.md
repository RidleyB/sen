In a world where other gods are distant and magic is policed, few call upon the **Collector** — a clandestine entity of death, debt, and unending crossings. The ritual to summon him is known by few, but those who do are offered power, for a price.

**His bargain is simple:**

> “For the length of your mortal life, I will lend you power. In return, you shall serve me that long again — as a reaper for the souls of the dead.”

The Dead God’s pacts are **ironclad, but deceptive**. He will often tack on additional tasks throughout your mortal life; refusing his demands can raise his ire; obeying them can earn reprieve. Most warlocks hide their brand, for Imperial Inquisitors burn such pact-holders on sight as heretics.

> *In play:* Warlocks of Mordt are bound to the **Collector’s Debt** — a cosmic contract of life, death, and obligation. Such bargains are taboo, resulting in warlocks being rare and seclusive.
