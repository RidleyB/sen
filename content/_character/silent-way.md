Monks of Mordt follow **the Silent Way** — a belief that the world itself still breathes beneath the silver light. They seek stillness to hear the **whispers of the wind, the pulse of stone, and the murmur of ash**. To them, movement and breath are sacred language — the last voice of a dying world.

Their monasteries, often built into cliffs or buried ruins, are tolerated only because the Empire sees them as harmless mystics. In truth, many monks sense the sickness of the land and hope to restore balance when the light finally dims.

> *In play:* Monks are spiritual anchors in a world that has forgotten how to listen. They may commune with the earth, feeling the influence of the world’s pain as a literal sickness.
