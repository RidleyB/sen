The Dust stretches across the southern cape of Nimyria, encompassing the continent’s lowest elevations. A cold, ashen desert, the region is cloaked in near-constant shadow, as the Dawnspire’s argent light barely reaches its sands. Winds howl across the plains, carrying fine gray sand and dust that obscures distant horizons. Travelers speak of the region as a place where light, warmth, and hope fade together, and where only the hardiest mortals survive.

<!--more-->

As the Dust rises toward the north, it forms a steep ravine separating the inner and outer provinces.

The Argent Road traverses the ravine, a raised and fortified route that spans the darkest portions of the Dust. Its path is illuminated by Argent Lanterns, crafted from silver-hued crystal to catch and reflect what little light reaches the region. To navigate the steep walls of the ravine, massive stone-and-iron elevators carry wagons, beasts, and travelers from the Dust up to the higher provinces- marvels of engineering and occasionally magical assistance, but dangerous in storms or when sabotaged by Imperial or rebel forces.

There are two stretches of such a road; one in the **central region**, the other **along the coast to the north**.

**Geography:**  
A barren sea of gray sand and cracked earth defines The Dust. The ground is laced with veins of pale glass, formed where lightning strikes the dry plains. The air carries a fine, stinging grit that seeps into lungs and cloth alike. Temperatures drop sharply at night, and the cold wind — constant and dry — whispers like distant voices. Occasional dunes rise like frozen waves, marking the remains of long-buried structures or roads that once led somewhere.
