Farshade stretches along the western coast of Nimyria, where the sea swallows the light and gives back only gray reflections. Salt, trade, and quiet resentment define life here. Farshade has always been the subject of neglect from the eastern empire. Its's people wish fruitlessly for rebellion, though lack and centralized force to drive it.

Here Nimyr's light shines dimmer, though still visible and able to account for the land's middling success in terms of agriculture and climate.

<!--more-->

**Geography:**  
Cliff-lined coasts and storm-tossed bays shape Farshade’s rugged beauty. The region is often veiled in mist, its air thick with the scent of brine and decay. Inland, rolling heaths and low forests hide ruins of the first Ascended settlements- now overrun by moss and rain. The sea crashes endlessly against black stone shores, said to echo the pulse of Nimyr’s dying light.
