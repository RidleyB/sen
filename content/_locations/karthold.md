Karthold is a cold, windswept island north of the mainland — the empire’s unspoken punishment ground. It serves as both labor colony and prison, where the condemned, the indebted, and the forgotten are sent to work until they break. Overseen by Ascended wardens, Karthold produces ore, stone, and salt for the empire’s endless needs. Few speak of it willingly; fewer return from it at all.

<!--more-->

**Geography:**  
The island is barren and hostile- jagged cliffs rising from a gray sea, its surface whipped constantly by icy rain. The soil is poisoned from years of mining runoff, and even moss struggles to grow. Settlements cling to the coasts in cramped, fortified enclaves, built more to keep workers in than dangers out. At night, the horizon burns faintly red from the forges — a ghostly glow that never quite fades.

**Places of Interest**

- **Oubliette:** A fortress-prison carved into the stone at the heart of Karthold, acting as the central hub to its inner workings.
