Wildrun is a vast and untamed island northeast of the mainland, home mainly to beastfolk — a race of half-intelligent, animalistic humanoids long since driven from the mainland. The island is a wilderness of tooth and instinct, where the empire holds virtually no sway, and nature itself seems unwilling to yield. Long ago the mainland featured these same beasts, but Nimyr's light and civilization drove them away.

<!--more-->

**Geography:**  
Dense cold jungles and moss-covered fens cover most of Wildrun, broken by deep valleys and rivers that flow between the hills. The air is heavy with a cold humidity. Ancient stone ruins lie buried beneath roots and vines — fragments of a civilization that may predate even Nimyr’s rise. Storms often circle the island’s edges, making approach by sea treacherous and unpredictable.
