Palor lies at the northern tip of Nimyria. A wind-beaten expanse where Nimyr’s silver light barely pierces the clouds. The people here are hard, proud, and weary of the empire that claims them but rarely aids them. Palor is a land of quiet resistance, surviving through grit more than grace.

<!--more-->

**Geography:**  
Jagged highlands and frozen moors dominate the Paloran landscape. The soil is poor and thin, and most settlements cling to the rocky coastlines where the sea still provides. Constant wind carries a low, whistling hum. Locals say it’s the voice of Nimyr, grown distant and cold. Nights are long and cold, while days bring only brief relief and dim misty light.

**Trivia & Notes:**  
- Palor’s people are known for their stoicism; it’s said a Paloran “would rather break than bend.”  
- The light of the Dawnspire appears as a dim, constant shimmer on the southern horizon; a distant light that never warms them.
