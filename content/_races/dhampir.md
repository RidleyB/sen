Those of the vampiric lineage are born or bitten of blood touched by the long-dead darkness of Thagar, manifesting traits both human and predatory. Their hunger is tempered, their strength formidable yet mortal, and they can walk in **[nimlight](../lore/calendar)** with care, though the dark calls to them always. Vampiric-born characters possess keen senses, unnatural reflexes, and an aura that unnerves both beasts and men, but must contend with a subtle, ever-present thirst and the societal stigma of their kind. They are the shadow children of Mordt, balancing survival, morality, and the seductive whisper of the night.
