Tiefling are the descendants of humans who accepted Nimyr’s gift centuries ago. Their infernal appearance, marked by horns, tails, and ashen or crimson skin, is both feared and admired. While some serve the Ascendants faithfully, others reject their heritage, striving to live on the fringes of Nimyria’s light. Tiefling inherit the subtle power of Nimyr’s blessing, whether as a spark of elemental fire, silvered charm, or other uncanny trait. Their cursed blood carries both privilege and peril: it is a mark of survival, a warning, and a bridge to the Ascendants’ immortal light.
