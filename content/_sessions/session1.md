### Party Introduction

The prisoners are returned to their group cell after another day of forced labor in the mines. Their food is moldy, the water stale, and their prospects bleak. The group consists of:

* *A large gray-skinned tiefling with piercing eyes and a quiet demeanor. An insatiable hunger keeps his fire burning.* **Victor.**
* *A hulking tiefling brooding in the corner, waiting for the others to finish eating so he can clean up.* **Seethe.**
* *A human thrall adorned in tattoos, attempting to accept his new and hopeless reality.* **Zeraph.**
* *A silenced Vincularis, bound at the mouth to prevent even the smallest cantrip.* **Creed.**
* *A jaded looking tiefling picking through the bread, who eats an insect for added protein.* **Sonny.**

### Riot’s Arrival

That evening, a new prisoner is thrown into the cell. He is a tiefling who has been beaten nearly to death. Victor drags the man into the shadows to feed, but Creed intervenes with telepathic magic. He rouses the stranger long enough for him to give his name: Riot. Victor decides ultimately not to feed on the man. On closer inspection, the man's knuckles bear two tattoos in stylized script that read RIOT and RUIN.

Later that night Sonny and Creed quietly consider an escape using picks and miner’s acid. They decide the plan is too risky for now.

{% include gallery %}

### The Execution March

The next morning the prisoners are woken in an unusual manner- the prison is louder and busier than usual, and they are referred to as "condemned", and marched upward toward the surface rather than into the mines like all the days before. Instead of the mines, they are brought into an execution courtyard. The perimeter is built as a stone theater where nobles and idle guards gather to watch prisoners die.

Among the crowd an Ascendant draws attention. They are a tiefling with long black hair that lightens at the ends and matching dark horns.

Zeraph is pushed forward first. The moment his shackles are undone he casts invisibility. Panic spreads as guards search for him. The Ascendant steps forward insisting that Zeraph’s execution be halted. When no one can find him the officials grow agitated and rush toward the courtyard floor.

### The Courtyard Collapse

While the chaos builds Seethe and Victor tackle a distracted guard. Another guard moves toward Riot who is barely standing. He signals urgently in Thieves Cant, a message Sonny sees in the chaos. The message is simple: get down.

An explosion tears through the courtyard. Stone, wood, and scaffolding collapse into an abandoned level of Karthold. Riot is buried under rubble with his leg crushed beyond saving. Beastmen begin to emerge from the surrounding darkness. These creatures have been twisted by years of isolation in the forgotten reaches of the prison.

The party fights off the Beastmen while the guard digs Riot free.

### Kael'ith and the Pale Banner

The guard reveals his name: Kael'ith. He is a member of the Pale Banner, and was involved in the attempt to free Riot. He offers the party a chance to escape the prison if they help him get Riot out alive.

Riot’s injured leg is amputated and cauterized by the party. Kaelith’s reaction makes it clear that he cares for Riot deeply.

{% include gallery id="gallery2" %}

### Into the Abandoned Prison

The group moves deeper through the ruined level of Karthold. They find a guard who had previously harassed them; Victor drains him without hesitation. They fight more Beastmen, and find some resources from a raised guard post. 

### Session End

The session concludes with the party resting in a small defensible outpost in the abandoned prison level. Riot is injured but stable, Beastmen roam the dark corridors, and Kael'ith marches with the survivors toward the promise of escape.
