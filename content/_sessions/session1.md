## The Oubliette

Deep beneath Karthold’s central fortress, a cold, wet oubliette cell where the condemned await execution. The air is thick with mildew, rot, and despair.

The party — **Zeraph, Sonny, Seethe, Victor, and Creed** — have been in the cell for several days, surviving on moldy bread and silence. The walls sweat, and the iron door hasn’t opened except to toss in scraps. Then, late one night, the guards drag in a new prisoner: **Riot**.

## Prison Cell: Before Dawn

The guards open the door with a scream of rust and throw a limp body inside.

“This one-” one spits, kicking the man's into the cell you're all in. “this lot is set for execution tomorrow.” The man hits the stone hard — wrists and ankles bound, beaten to near-unconsciousness. Riot fades into fitful sleep. 

When he wakes near dawn, his first words — weak, hoarse — are:

"Name's Riot- pleasure..."

Then silence- he falls back unconcious.

## The March to the Yard

The following morning, the clanging of boots and keys marks the arrival of the guards.

“Condemned! On your feet!”

The prisoners are chained together at the wrists, collars tightened, and marched up spiraling stone stairs toward the surface — toward the *light they haven’t seen in years*. The air grows colder and brighter as they emerge into the courtyard of the Oubliette.

## The Courtyard Execution

The sound of boots on stone echoes through the corridors as you’re marched forward, the smell of rust, blood, and rain growing thicker with each step. The guards say nothing. Chains rattle. Somewhere ahead, you can hear an array of voices- a crowd.

The tunnel opens into a broad courtyard, half ringed with scaffolds and rusted watchtowers. Above you, Silver Nimyrian banners hang limp in the wind. The ground is slick with ash and old blood. A wooden platform — the scaffold — dominates the center, surrounded by armored soldiers and onlookers.In front of the platform, a pile of heads lie scattered and bruised from when they fall from the scaffold above.

Those in the crowd are off-duty soliders, imperial officers, and a small collection of Ascendants in plainclothes; nobles. Here for either sick pleasure or to see justice in action.

You’re herded to a stop along the wall. From here, you can see several other prisoners already kneeling by the platform. One of them is being dragged up the stairs — a tall figure in a hood, wrists chained, limping slightly. They place him on the edge of the platform, and the Executioner sees that justice is swift. 

*Schwing*

The axe falls, and another head rolls off the scaffolding.

"Next."

Another prisoner is pushed forward, up the stairs. This one pushes against the soliders, but quickly subdued. 

You watch as each prisoner is brought up and swiftly put to death, thoughtless and cold. At the same time, you all begin to notice a minor commotion in the crowd- someone in the group of nobles is causing a fuss, arguing with some nearby soliders.

A soldiers standing by Zeraph pushes his shoulder suddenly and violently towards the scaffold.

"Walk."

He is motioned to walk towards the scaffold, to join the group of prisoners. 

As Zeraph marches across the yard, a man steps forward from the crowd of murmuring Ascendants, and begins walking down to enter the courtyard from the surrounding theatre.

"Stay the execution-"

The voice cuts across the courtyard. Eyes glance over, and the man separates from the crowd. A pale Ascendant man with black horns that curl downwards, long black hair that lightens at the ends. His piercing blue eyes are visible even across the courtyard. At his waist, he carries a large lantern that flickers faintly with a blue light.

"That one is my property- I intend to take him back with me."

He points a finger towards Zeraph. An official standing on the scaffold scowls towards the man, and marches towards the edge of the scaffold.

"Back in the crowd, sir- all these prisoners have long been condemned."

*Schwing*

Another head rolls into the courtyard. The man pushes past the soldier standing at the stairs to enter the courtyard. 

"I said, unshackle him- he is mine, he's coming home with ME-"

The officer on the platform rolls his eyes.

"*sighs* Someone stop him."

"Next."

The guard begins to push Zeraph up the scaffold. The man begins to walk faster towards them, kicking a head out of the way.

The soldier standing behind you looks distracted, glancing between the scaffolds. The soldier beside Victor walks towards the man approaching them, hand on his sword.

"I am not leaving until that man is in my property-"

"Sir, I'm telling you, back off-"

You see the soldier behind you make a gesture. Sonny, you recognize this as Thieves' Cant- he's signalling someonone.

"Get on the platform!" Another solider is pushing Zeraph.

"Zeraph, come to me, now." 

Suddenly, the entire courtyard erupts in a massive explosion- the surrounding theatre shifts as if struck by a sudden earthquake, before tons of stone and brick are hurled into the air. The dust and snow on the courtyard all shudders at once, before the sheer force of the blast begins caving in the entire area.

The signalling soldier watches the explosion, eyes wide in shock.

"Oh no-"

They dive towards the sixth prisoner, who has begun to fall from the sudden shifting beneath his feet.

Before you can see much else, the entire courtyard gives way beneath you. Darkness overtakes you, and the chaos of the moment disorients you beyond understanding.

## The Collapse Below

The first thing you feel is the weight.

Stone. Dust. The pressure of the world pressing down on you — and then, the air rushes back. You’re not dead. Somehow.

The room at the fringes is **pitch black**, save for the streaks of light bleeding through cracks in the rubble above. The air tastes like iron and smoke. The ringing in your ears makes it impossible to tell how long you’ve been unconscious.

Your restraints are broken and busted- the blast must’ve shattered the locks.

When you move, stone and bone crunch underfoot. Somewhere close, water drips steadily into a widening puddle. You can smell rot beneath the smoke — *not* the rot of death, but something older.

The silence breaks with a frantic voice nearby — **the solder from before**, clawing towards your cellmate who lies barely conscious, half-buried under broken masonry. His leg is twisted unnaturally beneath a slab of stone.

Further back, you can hear others shifting — groans, whispers, the scrape of iron. Some survived. Some didn’t.

Then — movement elsewhere. From a shadowed archway at the edge of the collapsed hall, you hear it: a dragging sound, wet and uneven. A ragged breath.

A faint, sickly glow pulses from deeper in the ruin — the collapsed tunnels of the **lower oubliette**, where the old fortress met the buried cells long forgotten.

You can make out half-rotted vines hanging from the ceiling, iron doors sealed with growth — *thorned vines*, black and sharp, coiling out of cracks in the walls like veins.

The smell of decay grows stronger as shapes begin to move beyond the faint light — thin, starved silhouettes, crawling over stone. Their eyes catch the dim glow like animals’.

One of them screeches, too human to be beast, too broken to be man.

Roll initiative.

* The players begin amid debris; movement is limited, forcing them to use improvised weapons or whatever’s nearby (chains, splintered planks, jagged stone).
* **Riot** is injured and pinned — cannot assist. He’s the first visual clue of the jailbreak’s real target.
* After combat, the players may find a **broken blade**, a **fallen torch**, or **keys on a dead guard** — early, tangible progress in the chaos.
* The beastmen are gaunt, feral remnants of prisoners warped by isolation and the creeping curse; their bodies covered in patches of black, thornlike growths.


### **Combat Encounter — Starved Beastmen**

**Setup:**
[Three or four half-feral humanoids](../creatures/starving-beastman) crawl out from the darkness — thin, bone-white, covered in patchy fur and open sores. Their eyes glow faint amber in the dark. They reek of damp fur and decay. Their movements are erratic, jerky, desperate.

* **Environment:**

  * Rubble piles grant cover.
  * Fires from the explosion create difficult terrain.
  * A broken guard corpse nearby offers a rusted short sword or spear.
  * One fallen lamp or torch provides dim light.

**Goal:** Survive. Drive off or kill the beastmen before they overwhelm Kael’ith and Riot.

During the fight:

* Kael’ith is preoccupied stabilizing Riot, shouting quick commands or prayers.
* The beastmen occasionally turn on the wounded guards, showing their animal hunger.
* Victor’s hunger, Seethe’s instincts, or Creed’s chained tome may react — use this to give each player an eerie, personal beat mid-combat.

Once the last beastman falls, the silence afterward is deafening — broken only by Riot’s groan of pain and Kael’ith’s sharp breathing.

### Aftermath — “Ash in the Blood”

Kael’ith pulls the broken mask away, revealing the silver-veined skin and faintly luminous eyes of an Ascendant. He meets their gaze — exhausted, blood running from his temple.

“I think he's stable...we need to find a way out-” the guard looks up to all of you, taking a long look at each of you for the first time. He pauses, glancing down towards your cellmate.

"Thank you- it's a miracle you all survived the blast."

"I just meant to cause a distraction- not collapse the whole damn place!"
Riot wheezes a laugh through cracked lips:

“You always did overdo it, Ashblood.”
"Riot!"
Kael’ith looks down to him, holding his face gently.
"You're awake."
"'Fuck did you get us into?"
"The Banner helped me free you- we're getting you home. I have a cache hidden in the countryside. Food, medicine, weapons… maybe enough to reach a ship before Karthold regains its footing.”

"Kael'ith looks up at all of you, and sighs."

“I can’t drag him and fight. Help me get him out, and I’ll make sure you live to see the sun again. That’s all I can promise.”

This gives the party a clear direction and a reason to cooperate: **help Kael’ith save Riot, escape through the Forsaken Cells, reach the surface.**

## The Guard in the Rubble

As you pick your way through the shattered corridor, the air grows thick with dust and the acrid tang of smoke. Stone and timber litter the floor, making each step uncertain.

Ahead, something shifts — a low, ragged groan echoing from beneath a half-collapsed wall. Moving closer, you see him: a man in tattered armor, one leg pinned under a chunk of stone. His face is smeared with blood, sweat, and dirt, but you recognize him instantly — the guard who taunted Victor.

He coughs, his voice weak and rasping:

“Help… me… please… damn it...”

His sword lies just out of reach, bent and useless. A few dark stains on his chest suggest he won’t last long without aid. 

## The Silent Hallway

The corridor ahead narrows. Broken beams and jagged stone litter the floor, mingled with fragments of bone and shattered armor. The faint, wet scuttle of movement echoes off the walls — far away, but unmistakable.

You realize that any careless step could alert whatever lurks nearby. From the shadows, glowing red eyes seem to watch, waiting for the smallest sound.

You need to move **carefully through the corridor** to avoid attracting attention. Every misstep risks summoning more starved beastmen from the darkness.

---

### Skill Challenge: Treading Carefully

* **Goal:** Move 50 feet through the corridor without alerting the beastmen.
* **Rounds:** 3–5 “rounds” of movement, depending on speed. Each round, describe a segment of the corridor with new hazards.
* **Success Threshold:** 3–4 successes before 2 failures, depending on the number of checks.

### **Checks and DCs**

| Check Type           | DC | Effect on Success                                                                                | Effect on Failure                                                                                             |
| -------------------- | -- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Perception (Wis)** | 13 | Spot loose debris, unstable beams, bones that crunch underfoot. Avoids hazard and reduces noise. | Step on hidden bone or twig, roll d4 damage (1–4) and alert nearby beastmen (adds 1d4 + 1 enemies to combat). |
| **Stealth (Dex)**    | 14 | Move silently across rubble. Success prevents beastmen from hearing you.                         | Step causes noise; beastmen begin stalking from the shadows.                                                  |
| **Survival (Wis)**   | 12 | Identify safest path through debris, noting which sections might collapse or hide enemies.       | Choose poorly; hazard triggers minor collapse (1d6 bludgeoning, potential for a minor ambush).                |
| **Athletics (Str)**  | 15 | Climb over or push aside heavy debris without disturbing the floor.                              | Causes thump and vibration, attracting attention (1 extra beastman appears).                                  |

Optional: Give **advantage** on the first check for PCs who examine the corridor closely or spend 10 seconds observing before moving.

**Environmental Hooks**
* Cracked walls with black thorned brambles. Touching them causes minor damage (1d4) and warns the beastmen of intrusion.
* Pools of stagnant water can hide bones or debris; stepping in may create splashes that alert predators.
* Hanging chains or broken beams above: failure can make them fall, causing sound or damage.

**Consequences**
* **Too many failures:** All lurking beastmen (3–6) are immediately aware and rush in for combat.
* **All successes:** The party traverses quietly, reaching a **small alcove or collapsed guard station** without alerting enemies. This gives them a chance to **rest, loot, or plan**.
* **Mixed success:** Beastmen hear some noise but are cautious — some may stalk from shadows, others may investigate only if they sense movement.

## The Raised Guard Post

After navigating the dark, narrow corridor, you reach a section of the prison that rises slightly above the surrounding rubble — a **crumbling guard post**, its stone foundation cracked and dusted with soot. The air here is thicker, heavy with the smell of mold, rot, and old blood.

Broken beams lean against the walls. Rusted hooks jut from the ceiling. The faint light from above filters through holes in the collapsed roof, illuminating a few **mouldering crates**, splintered and rotting. Some are empty; others contain scraps of old armor, dented helmets, and cracked flasks with unknown liquids.

At the far end of the raised platform, partially hidden beneath a fallen plank, you notice a **wooden chest**. Its lid is slightly warped, but the lock is intact. Inside, you find a few items clearly left behind by soldiers long gone:

* **A pair of short swords**, dulled but serviceable.
* **A few coils of rope** and a lantern with some oil remaining.
* **A small pouch of coins**, tarnished but usable.
* **A damaged lute**, strings snapped and wood cracked along one side. Despite its state, the lute’s frame is solid — with some ingenuity, it could serve as a spellcasting implement.

The chest also contains scraps of parchment — crude sketches of the prison layout and hastily written notes, perhaps left by previous escapees or soldiers.

The raised post gives a good view of the corridor leading back the way you came, and a vantage point for watching the shadows beyond. The black thorned brambles creep close to the edges, twisting between the stone.

* **Sonny** can attempt to repair or improvise the lute as a spellcasting focus (Arcana or Sleight of Hand DC 12–15).
* **All players** can scavenge weapons or gear from the crates/chest. Armor scraps may provide light improvised protection; swords may be slightly easier to use than rubble as weapons.
* **Perception / Investigation** (DC 13–15) may reveal small hidden compartments in the chest or under loose floorboards — more coins, potions, or crude rations.
* **Environmental Hooks:** Thorned brambles at the edges of the raised post may snag clothing or armor, or deal 1d4 piercing if brushed carelessly.

## Collapse into the Caves

Beyond the raised guard post, the corridor narrows sharply. Chunks of masonry and jagged beams form a jagged barricade, the walls leaning like they might fall at any moment. The air is thick, musty, and heavy with dust and smoke. You can feel the faint tremor of the earlier explosion in your bones, a persistent hum of instability.

As you step forward, the floor gives way beneath you. Stone and rubble tumble away. You lurch, catching yourself on a broken beam, and realize that the prison itself has fractured — the walls ahead are gone entirely, opening into a **dark cavity below**.

Cold, damp air rushes upward, carrying the scent of earth and mineral, a stark contrast to the iron and rot of the prison. Water trickles faintly from somewhere unseen, echoing through the chamber with an irregular drip.

Jagged stone surrounds you on all sides, forming a natural chute leading down into a **subterranean cave system**. Old mining tracks, half-collapsed wooden supports, and abandoned tools hint that this area was once used by laborers long before the prison was built. Shadows twist and stretch in the dim light, making shapes that may or may not be alive.

Faint sounds reach you: the scrape of claw on stone, the drip of water into pools, the distant echo of collapsing tunnels further down. Somewhere below, the faint glimmer of phosphorescent mineral catches your eye, reflecting off slick rock like distant stars.

You realize this cave system could serve as both a **way out of the prison** and a potential **dangerous labyrinth**, filled with unstable rock, starving beastmen, and the remnants of abandoned mines.

**Narrative Hooks / Environmental Features**

* **Cave Path**: Twisting tunnels, occasional loose rocks that might fall if stepped on or disturbed.
* **Abandoned Mining Gear**: Rusted pickaxes, wheelbarrows, tracks that may still hold remnants of carts — could be used as improvised weapons or tools.
* **Water & Light**: Dripping pools and faintly glowing minerals can provide both illumination and hazards (slippery floors).
* **Ambient Sounds**: Echoes of collapsing stone, distant growls, and the whisper of unseen creatures heighten tension.
* **Escape & Exploration Choice**: Players can move carefully to avoid collapse, scout for easier paths, or rush and risk alerting nearby threats.

Here’s how you can narrate and structure this section of the dungeon based on your map and goals. The flow moves naturally from the **collapsed entry point (bottom right)** toward the **coastal outflow (top left)**, weaving in tension, opportunity, and narrative reinforcement.

## Into the Mines

The air grows cooler and wetter as you descend the fractured tunnel. Your boots scrape over broken stone and twisted iron, the remnants of old prison infrastructure jutting from the earth like ribs. Drips echo faintly in the dark, mingling with the slow pulse of water flowing somewhere nearby.

The flicker of light from your torches reveals a network of **rough-hewn tunnels**, clearly **once mined** but long since reclaimed by the dark. You can make out **carved supports**, **rusted rails**, and a **thin channel of greenish water** flowing through one side — **the mine runoff** that snakes deeper into the rock.

---

## Branching Paths

As the party progresses, they come upon a **junction of caverns** — several branching channels twisting away from the main runoff.

* The **central path** follows the **runoff**, winding slowly **upward** toward fresher air — the **way out**.
* The **side paths** descend further into **dark, uncharted tunnels**, where the echoes are deeper and the air turns stale.
* Small bones, scraps of fur, and claw marks along the stone walls suggest the **beastmen** prowl the lower tunnels.

If players investigate or wander off the main path, they’ll sense:

The faint sound of breathing. Wet. Animal.
The scrape of bone on stone.

It’s clear that **further in means danger**, while **following the runoff** feels faintly cleaner, fresher — a lifeline toward freedom.

---

## Beastmen Ambush (Avoidable Encounter)

As you pass through a narrow cavern, your light catches the glint of eyes — a low, guttural hiss breaks the silence.

Three **Beastmen scavengers** lurk near the runoff, **feeding on a corpse** or **gnawing on bones** from old miners.

* If the players **move quietly** (DC 13 Stealth), they can **slip by unseen** — the creatures too distracted by their meal.
* A failed check draws their attention: snarling, they rise, bone weapons in hand, scrambling over the rocks.
* If engaged, the fight is close and claustrophobic; the uneven terrain and narrow space make movement limited.

**Stats:** use **Gnoll Witherlings** or **custom Beastmen** with 11 AC, 9 HP, Multiattack (bite/claw).

Optional Kael’ith interference — a faint *crack of blue energy* deeper in the tunnels, startling the beasts mid-fight, giving the players an opening to flee.

## Cave Outflow

> Beyond the supply room, the runoff widens into a jagged tunnel. The walls glisten with green light reflecting off the water.
> The sound of waves grows faintly audible now — a rhythmic pulse beyond the stone. The smell of salt creeps in.

As the party pushes on:

* The air grows cleaner, colder.
* Beastmen tracks thin out.
* Broken beams and rusted rails give way to rough, natural stone.

At the tunnel’s end, a final climb — slick and steep — opens into the **coastal outflow**, where daylight filters through cracks in the stone.

> You emerge to the sound of distant surf — pale light bleeding through a ragged hole in the cliffside. The runoff pours out in a green stream into the sea below. The air tastes like salt and freedom.

## Arrival at Kael’ith’s Safehouse (Session End)

The long climb through the runoff tunnels ends at a break in the stone where cold, salt-heavy wind cuts through the air. You push out through a narrow grate bent by the explosion’s shockwave. Beyond it, the coast opens wide — jagged black cliffs, the distant crash of waves, and the lights of a small port flickering a few kilometers north.

The place is quiet. Cargo cranes sit motionless. Lanterns sway. Even from here you can hear gulls and the low rumble of longshoremen finishing their shifts.

Kael’ith leads you along the cliff edge, keeping low, stopping whenever the wind picks up or patrol torches sweep across the dark. Eventually he brings you to a squat shack half-buried in the stone. Driftwood patched the cracks in the walls. A rusted chimney leans like it’s given up.

He pushes the door open.

Inside: a single room. A battered table. A cold stove. A cot that’s seen too many damp nights. Shelves stacked with loose bandages, salted fish, a few tools, and a worn cloak or two — nothing valuable, everything functional. The smell of sea brine and old oil clings to everything. Kael’ith shuts the door behind you.

He lifts a few floorboard back to reveal his true supplies; weapons, armor, enough to get started.

“You’ll be safe here,” he says. “For tonight. Patrols don’t sweep this stretch often.”

He drops to a knee beside Riot, checking his breathing.

“I’ll keep watch. Rest while you can. Tomorrow, we talk about getting you off this island.”

Outside, the wind batters the boards. Inside, for the first time since the scaffold, the world stops trying to kill you.

**Session ends.**
