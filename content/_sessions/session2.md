# Session 2: Riot II

### Into the Abandoned Depths

Leaving the raised guard post behind, the party explored deeper into the forgotten corridors. The path soon split in two. After some deliberation, they chose the western route, guided by a faint draft Seethe had detected coming from the cavernous sections ahead.

### The Runoff Chambers

The route led beneath the sluice runoff system where processed ore waste is flushed toward the sea. The explosion had ruptured pipes and machinery, flooding the chamber with dripping acid. Seethe advanced first to chart a route through the pools but was attacked immediately as acidic oozes fell from the ceiling.
Once the creatures were destroyed, the group pressed on and reached a sloped corridor coated in slime. As they attempted to cross, two large oozes on opposite ends began sliding inward to crush them.

### Overcoming the Oozes

A lever halfway down the slope became their salvation. Victor, Creed, and Sonny forced it open, diverting part of the acidic flow. The surge washed away the lower ooze, while the upper one dropped through the grating once it was exposed.

### The Last Outpost

Deeper in the ruin they found an abandoned supply post containing a ledger and remnants of old smuggling operations. Among the supplies were a damaged greatsword, a small lockbox of silver coins, and a decayed journal describing illicit trades between smugglers and prison guards for mining tools.

### Escape to the Coast

At the end of the tunnels the party discovered an outflow channel that emptied onto the rocky shoreline. After descending to the beach, Kaelith guided them toward a countryside safehouse. There he revealed the supplies he had prepared and instructed the group to rest before continuing their escape from Karthold the next morning.
