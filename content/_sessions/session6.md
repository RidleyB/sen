## Overview
Following the interrogation of Redwake, the party rested at the Old Keelhouse for the evening. The following morning they set out northeast from Blacktide, travelled for half the day, and came upon the warehouse. After some reconnaissance, the party hatched a plan to distract the guards of the warehouse while Zeraph crept around while invisible, dousing the lights. 

The session ended with the howl of Beastmen approaching, looking to hunt their newly shadowed prey.

## A Minor Detour
Partway through their journey, the party happened upon an overturned cart, stripped and abandoned. A dessicated corpse lay at it's feet. This turned out to have been an ambush by a group of Bramble Stalkers, who also tried to attack the party. 

After a fast but furious encounter, the party stood victorious, and moved on.

## Lying in Wait
Upon arrival of the shadowed warehouse, the party learned that it was occupied; more than a dozen smugglers were guarding the warehouse, and the party considered how to proceed going forward. Ultimately, they decided on attempting to douse the lamps that protected the guards from the darkness, keeping the Beastmen at bay.

Sonny conjured an illusion of dancing lights to begin their distraction, while Zeraph went invisible and approached each of the lanterns surrounding the camp. He moved swiftly, deftly dousing each lamp surrounding the warehouse, and the camp began to erupt into chaos. 

After the last border lamp was doused, only leaving the bonfire in the center of the camp, a long, dreadful howl echoed through the surrounding forest- the beastmen approach.

## Next Session
A threat is fast approaching... for the party, for the smugglers. Run, hide, or fight. The choice is theirs.
