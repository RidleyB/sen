## Overview

The party rests overnight at the safehouse. Victor and Seethe remain outside, with Seethe meditating amid scorched grass, while Kaelith keeps watch. By morning, Kaelith explains that he'll need to renegotiate their safe passage off Karthold; their initial deal was only for he and Riot. The smuggler involved is Captain Veylan, whom the party will need to meet and barter their passage as well. Riot is left behind to recover as the party travels toward Black Tide, though the parting is tense.

### Blacktide

Taking side roads, the group reaches the port town of Blacktide. Kaelith attempts to meet Captain Veylan alone, but quickly realizes the party will not stay uninvolved (**Sonny**). While he negotiates, the group explores the town. Creed, Zeraph, and Victor visit a magic shop run by a senile woman who offers goods in exchange for a favor, while Seethe smokes and observes the streets. When the group reunites, Kaelith reports that Captain Veylan wants to meet them all.

### Captain Veylan’s Job

Inside, the party meets Captain Veylan, who offers drinks before explaining the problem. A magically sealed lockbox has gone missing from one of his pier-side warehouses. Only his crew knew of its existence, leading him to suspect an internal theft. He directs the party to investigate the warehouse and its ledger, naming his boatswain Grit as someone who knows the operation well. Once they identify the culprit, they are to bring the lockbox to him, and he will decide what is to be done.

After the meeting, Kaelith returns to the safehouse to watch over Riot, while the party remains in Blacktide.

### The Rotting Ship

Before pursuing the lockbox, the party decides to complete the favor for the magic shopkeeper. Following her directions, they reach a decaying ship preyed-on by Beastmen and overgrown with Violet Fungi. Through careful use of light and intimidation, they avoid a direct confrontation. Seethe gathers what little usable fungus remains after the Violet Fungi destroy most of it, leaving them with only a single large specimen.

### Loose Ends

Returning to the shop, the group convinces the forgetful shopkeeper to part with a scroll of Detect Magic and a spyglass engraved with the initials V.N. She struggles to remember the details of the exchange, and Zeraph impulsively gives his name when asked. The party leaves with their rewards, reminded that caution is often necessary when dealing with strangers.
