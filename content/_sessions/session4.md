## Overview

The party continues the search for the missing lockbox that Captain Veylan had tasked them to find. They first began their search by splitting up; Creed, Victor, and Zeraph headed to the last known whereabouts of the lockbox, while Sonny and Seethe decided to visit the Scuttle's Lantern to ask the crew what they knew.

## The Warehouse

The trio found the warehouse, and investigated it's contents. They found evidence of the theft, some sooty footprints indicating something explosive or incendiary occurred, and drag marks showing the lockbox was pulled in a direction opposite to the other footprints.

Victor questioned some people outside, who turned out to be stationed guards patrolling the warehouse following the theft. Zeraph found a ledger, containing information that Grit, the Pale Wench's bosun, was one of the few who knew of the lockbox's whereabouts, hidden in this warehouse.

## The Scuttle's Lantern

Seethe and Sonny entered the tavern and spotted the crew of the Pale Wench- Captain Veylan's crew. A few spotted them as they entered, a few gave them some questioning looks. After speaking with Siren the barkeep, and two others by the bar, it seemed like they were met with resistance. Sonny tried to make friends with a musician on the crew, Lysa, but failed to locate his musical talent that particular evening.

## Confrontation

With what the party gathered, they figured questioning Grit would be their next course of action. Boarding the ship moored on the pier, the party was met with immediate confrontation from Grit, who ordered them off the ship. The party decided to hold their ground, and after a brief display of force and some magical trickery, Grit was charmed to compliance.
