## Overview
The party nears closer to the true location of the lockbox, springing an ambush on the rat Redwake who is behind the theft of the lockbox. After some interrogation, Redwake revealed that the new Quartermaster of the Blackfish Runners planned the job, and where to potentially find the lockbox.

## Standoff
The party stands on the deck of the Pale Wench, surrounded by a few deckhands, and the now charmed Grit, diffusing an otherwise tough situation. Grit explains that he was tipped off the lockbox was going to be stolen, and attempted to remove it's contents before anyone else could. He burned his hands, and fled the scene. Caught in his foolish actions, Grit remands himself to the party and faces Captain Veylan's judgement.

Standing before Veylan, the captain orders his first mate Temperance to cuff Grit and lock him in the brig aboard the Pale Wench. Grit offered some intel on how to find Redwake, which gave the party an idea for ambush.

## Ambush the Ambushers
The party ventured over to Grit's shanty hut off on the pier, and set up an ambush in wait for Redwake. Eventually he appeared, drunk, along with a smattering of other Blackfish sailors. They proved no match for the party, and were battered and bruised in a flash of combat.

After some intimidation and interrogation, Redwake spills that Viscera, the Blackfish's Quartermaster was behind the job, along with sharing the potential location of the lockbox- a shadowed warehouse further inland.
