*The Guild of Discovery is a coalition of explorers, scholars, and adventurers committed to venturing beyond the known and charting uncharted worlds. Within the Guild, Crash Teams form the vanguard of exploration. Small, highly skilled teams deployed to newly discovered, unstable, or hazardous planes. Their missions vary widely: exploration, recovery, first contact with new civilizations, and more. While these assignments offer great rewards, they carry equally great risk, and are not for the faint of heart.*

### THE WORLD OF KAVROS
Once, Kavros was a world of beauty and abundance. Its people mastered the elements, bending them to their will. But their ambition was boundless, and in their hunger for more power, they tore open the foundations of their world.
From the wound came Pyrakos, the First Flame — a titan of living fire, born from the planet’s molten heart. In a final act of defiance, the last of Kavros’ people bound the titan beneath the world in chains of iron and sorcery. The victory cost them everything.

Now, centuries later, the land above lies broken and ashen. Black glass plains stretch to the horizon. The sky burns dull red. The air itself carries the memory of fire. The dead do not rest — their souls twist in the lingering heat, bound to the embers of what they once were.

And deep below, the flame still stirs.

### THE CALL TO DESCEND
Recently, the Guild of Discovery augurs reported violent heat surges and tremors from beneath the massive seal where Pyrakos is believed to be entombed. 

### YOUR MISSION:
- **Descend** through the outer ruins of the tomb.
- **Assess** the integrity of the ancient wards.
- **Restore or reinforce** the seal if possible.
- **Return alive** to report structural status.

**If Pyrakos wakes, the fire that ended this world may spread beyond it.**

### WHAT YOU KNOW
- **The Surface:** Endless ash, skeletal ruins, rivers of half-cooled magma. The sun never breaks through the haze.
- **The Tomb:** Melted halls, runes burned into stone, heat thick enough to warp metal.
- **The Dead:** Fire-touched husks and wraiths of ash, still guarding the sacred halls.
- **The Seal:** A vast iron gate marked by a circular glyph, cracked and faintly glowing from within.
