The trail leads the adventurers to a massive obsidian structure: the so-called Crown. This is where the first team's final transmissions originated, and where the truth of Plibt begins to reveal itself.

## Approaching the Crown
*Play "Lair", by Joel Nielsen*
{: .notice--info}

**Read Aloud:**
*The wall of black glass looms above you, sheer and unbroken save for a near geometrically perfect opening near the base. A large triangular channel drives downwards deeper into Plibt and the Obsidian structure. In the channel, ooze flows like a river, pouring into the dark below.*

*The air grows humid, thick with the copper tang of ooze. The passage ahead is not stone, but a channel of viscous slime flowing sluggishly between black crystalline ribs. It pulses like veins, walls flexing faintly with each step you take. The ground trembles underfoot as though the structure is alive — shifting to swallow you whole if you misstep.*

### Skill Challenge

**DC:** 14 for targeted skills, disadvantage for unrelated ones.<br>
**Complexity:** 3 successes per player before 2 failures.<br>
**On Success:** The group navigates safely, emerging deeper inside the Crown.<br>
**On Failure:** One or more characters slip beneath the ooze, briefly submerged in a crystalline undercurrent. Each affected PC takes 2d6 bludgeoning damage and makes a Con save DC 14. On a failure, gain 1 tick of Unhappening (Ebb) as ooze clings unnaturally to their skin and whispers their own name back at them.

- **Athletics/Acrobatics:** Force through or nimbly cross unstable surfaces.
- **Survival/Nature:** Spot currents of ooze that flow quickly predicting where the channel shifts.
- **Perception:** Hear or see the faint cracking as crystal erupts ahead, giving warning before collapse.

*As you step through, the temperature begins to drop. The air hums faintly, vibrating in your chest like a second heartbeat. The floor is slick with a faint dusting of crystalline powder, each step ringing with a note that echoes far too long. The further you walk, the less it feels like you’re inside a structure, and more like you’ve entered the body of something vast, patient, and aware.*

*Inside, light refracts strangely — shadows lag behind movements, voices echo in chords rather than single tones.*

### Crystalline Mist

**Read Aloud:**
*The channel opens into a cavern where crystalline spires rise like teeth. Between them, a faint mist clings to the air — glittering particles that catch in your throat and eyes with every breath. The mist moves with intent, pouring from fissures in the walls, refracting light in sharp rainbow arcs. Standing still, you hear a faint chorus: your own voices, repeated in a hundred tones, layered until they become unbearable.*

**Mechanics:**

Each round in the mist, PCs must make a **Constitution Save DC 15**.
<br>
**On Success:** Irritated eyes/throat, but no effect.<br>
**On Failure:** Gain 1 tick of Resonance.<br>
**Investigation/Arcana DC 14:** Reveals crystalline vents in the walls dispersing the mist.

**Options:**
- **Destroy the Vents:** Requires Athletics DC 16 to smash or Arcana DC 14 to overload. Doing so clears the mist but awakens nearby passive crystalline guardians. Two Stone Defender (MME) or scaled Helmed Horrors pull themselves from the walls to engage.
- **Careful Crossing:** Group Stealth/Survival DC 13 to move quickly between mist pockets without stirring heavier concentrations.

Each failure forces another round of saves vs. Resonance.
