## Ooze Unhappening Track

At **8 ticks**, the character is fully assimilated into the Ebb, dissolving into a Plasmoid echo.

| Ticks | Stage               | Effect                                                                 |
|-------|---------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1     | Faint Resonance     | Reflection sometimes moves out of sync. Gain resistance to Psychic damage.      |
| 2     | Resonant Body	      | Your body resonates with the world around you. **Disadvantage on Stealth checks.**  |
| 3     | Fluid Mind          | Whispers edge into your thoughts. **Advantage on Insight checks**, but **disadvantage on Concentration saves.** |
| 4     | Partial Melt        | Your limbs become semi-fluid. **Disadvantage on Strength checks/saves. Advantage on checks to escape grapples.** |
| 5     | Ooze Hunger         | You have disadvantage on all skill checks except Insight. You cannot digest normal food; must consume liquid/ooze-like matter. Long rests without this only recover half HP/HD. |
| 6     | Fluid Body          | Your body briefly warps between solid and semi-fluid. **Attacks against you have advantage**, but **you can move through spaces as if you were one size smaller**. |
| 7     | Ooze-Form           | Your body becomes partly translucent, bones softening to fluid. **Resistance to bludgeoning damage** and **you can squeeze through any opening at least 6 inches wide**, but suffer 2d10 force damage as your form returns to normal. |
| 8     | Assimilation        | Collapse into fluid mass, absorbed into the Ebb. Character is lost.   |

## Crown Resonance Track

At **8 ticks**, the character is fully assimilated into the Crown, crystallizing into a hybrid husk.

| Ticks | Stage                  | Effect                                                                 |
|-------|------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1     | Subtle Glimmer          | Faint crystalline shimmer beneath the skin. Gain resistance to Psychic damage.
| 2     | Crystal Dust            | Fine glitter trails from your hair/skin. **Disadvantage on Stealth checks.** |
| 3     | Resonant Mind           | Your mind races while your body slows. **Advantage on Intelligence checks**, but **disadvantage on Dexterity saves.** |
| 4     | Hardened Flesh          | Limbs partially encased in crystal. **Resistance to slashing damage**, but **speed reduced by 10 ft.** |
| 5     | Splintered Will         | Shards of crystal protrude from your skull and arms. Disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws, but advantage on Strength saving throws and checks (the crystalline reinforcement makes you harder to move or control, but erodes your mental resistance). |
| 6     | Fragile                 | Your body hardens like glass. **Gain resistance to Piercing and Slashing damage**. **Gain vulnerability to bludgeoning, force, and thunder damage.** |
| 7     | Fractured Self          | Glimpses of yourself moving in jagged delay. **Enemies have disadvantage on ranged attacks against you. You have disadvantage on melee attack rolls.** |
| 8     | Assimilation            | Body crystallizes entirely, shattering into a Crown hybrid. Character is lost. |



## Scattered Items from Expedition Team Voss-3

* **Guild-Issued Survival Pack:** A waterproof satchel containing dried rations, 10 climbing pitons, 50ft coil of silk rope.
* **Ooze-Slick Flask:** A steel flask half-filled with a viscous liquid that smells faintly herbal. *Effect:* A DC 12 Medicine or Arcana check reveals it’s a poultice designed to counteract ooze burns. Drinking it grants **resistance to acid damage** for 1 hour. 1 use only.
* **Personal Charm (Seris Dain):** A halfling-sized bronze pendant etched with a stylized map. Worth little, but faint divination sigils flicker on it.
  * *Effect:* Once per day, grants advantage on a Survival check for navigation.

* **Guild Medical Kit:** A sturdy steel case containing salves, bandages, and strange-smelling draughts. *Effect:* Functions as 3x **Potions of Healing**.
* **Resonant Chisel (Brannic’s Tool):** A heavy iron chisel with a crystalline core. Designed to chip obsidian samples. *Effect:* Counts as a simple melee weapon (1d6 bludgeoning), dealing an extra 1d6 thunder damage to crystalline creatures. On a natural 20, it fractures crystal with resonant shock, killing crystalline creatures outright.

### Expedition Team Voss-3 (The Lost Crash)

**Captain Elorial Voss**

* **Race/Class:** Human Fighter (Eldritch Knight)
* **Guild Background:** Captain
* **Calling:** Duty
* **Backstory:** A decorated veteran of several Crash missions, Voss was known for her unshakable composure and precise leadership. Her reputation in the Guild was sterling — which makes his sudden “turning” even more unsettling.

**Envoy Talis Vicero**

* **Race/Class:** Half-Elf Bard (College of Eloquence)
* **Guild Background:** Envoy
* **Calling:** Understanding
* **Backstory:** Charismatic and endlessly curious, Talis believed every mind could be bridged through empathy. On Plibt, he became convinced the psychic pressure of the Ebb was not hostile but *invitational* — a conviction that grew dangerous as others began to resist.

**Researcher Sybil Erthea**

* **Race/Class:** Tiefling Wizard (Divination)
* **Guild Background:** Researcher
* **Calling:** Knowledge
* **Backstory:** Coldly pragmatic, Sybil saw Plibt as a treasure trove of arcane anomalies. She focused on documenting the shifting terrain and crystal resonance patterns, caring little for first contact. Privately, she distrusted Talis’s optimism.

**Security Officer Brannic Kael**

* **Race/Class:** Goliath Paladin (Oath of the Watchers)
* **Guild Background:** Security Officer
* **Calling:** Protection
* **Backstory:** A hulking wall of discipline, Brannic had one task — keep the scientists alive. He grew increasingly frustrated as the team splintered between caution and curiosity, and was last seen holding the line at Beta Camp.

**Pioneer Seris Dain**

* **Race/Class:** Halfling Ranger (Horizon Walker)
* **Guild Background:** Pioneer
* **Calling:** Discovery
* **Backstory:** The first to tread into danger, Seris marked trails and set camps with tireless precision. Known for her optimism and quick wit, her journals suggest she was the first to notice the terrain shifting beneath them — but her warnings weren’t heeded in time.

**Artisan-Linguist Verdigris Fen**

* **Race/Class:** Dragonborn Sorcerer (Aberrant Mind)
* **Guild Background:** Envoy (specialized)
* **Calling:** Influence
* **Backstory:** Though technically an envoy-in-training, Jorah had a gift for deciphering alien languages. His psionic talents made him sensitive to Plibt’s whispers — too sensitive. His fragmented notes reveal he may have been the first to “hear” the Crown.

## **Glittering Ooze**

*Medium Ooze, Unaligned*

AC: 8  
HP: 45 (6d8+18)  
Speed: 10 ft.  

STR 15 (+2) | DEX 6 (-2) | CON 16 (+3) | INT 2 (-4) | WIS 8 (-1) | CHA 1 (-5)  

Damage Resistances: Acid, Psychic  
Condition Immunities: Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Prone  
Senses: Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this), Passive Perception 9  
Languages: —  
CR: 2 (450 XP)  

---  

**Amorphous.** The ooze can move through a space as narrow as 1 inch wide without squeezing.  

**Resonant Death Burst.** When the ooze dies, it explodes in crystalline dust. Each creature within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 13 Con save or take 5 (1d10) psychic damage and gain 1 tick of *Resonance*.  

**Pseudopod.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
Hit: 10 (2d6+2) bludgeoning damage + 7 (2d6) acid damage.

## **Ooze Sinkholes (Hazard)**

*Large Environmental Hazard, Chaotic Neutral*

AC: —  
HP: —  
Trigger: Moving across unstable ooze pools  
Complexity: 3 successes before 2 failures (per character)  

Relevant Skills / DCs:  
- Survival / Nature (DC 14): Spot ripples or color shifts that indicate collapse.  
- Acrobatics (DC 14): Leap between stable ridges.  
- Athletics (DC 14): Haul a sinking ally free.  
- Perception (DC 14): Notice faint glimmers of crystal beneath ooze before collapse.  
- Arcana / Religion (DC 14): Recognize the ooze’s rejection of Crown intrusion.  

Complications (Failure):  
- Character sinks into a hole (restrained, 10 ft. down, acid damage 1d10/round).  
- Crystal shard erupts: 2d6 piercing damage to all in 10 ft.  
- Inhaled fumes: Disadvantage on next check.  

Success Outcome: Party crosses safely, learns crystalline intrusion is destabilizing terrain.  
Failure Outcome: Party loses resources or takes damage; exposed characters gain 1 tick of Resonance (dust exposure).

- Character resists, but voice lingers.

## **Crystal Sinkhole (Hazard)**

*Large Environmental Hazard, Lawful Neutral*

AC: —  
HP: —  
Trigger: Party crosses obsidian-veined terrain collapsing into caverns below.  
Complexity: 3 successes before 2 failures (per character).  

Relevant Skills / DCs:  
- Acrobatics (DC 14): Balance or leap across shards.  
- Athletics (DC 14): Climb a crumbling slope or pull ally up.  
- Arcana (DC 15): Identify crystal growth pattern and predict collapse.  
- Perception (DC 13): Spot safe footing before it breaks.  
- Investigation (DC 15): Find the cause of collapse (dust channels feeding Crown).  

Complications (Failure):  
- Character falls: 2d6 bludgeoning + 1d6 piercing damage.  
- Cloud of dust: DC 13 Con save or gain 1 tick of Resonance.  
- Obsidian shards pin item: lose a weapon/pack unless rescued.  

Success Outcome:  
- Party identifies crystal growth fueling collapse, safe passage across.  
- DM Clue: Terrain collapse is part of the Crown’s cycle of consumption.  

Failure Outcome:  
- Injuries and lost gear.  
- Players recognize geological similarity to ooze caverns → foreshadowing the cycle.

## **Crystalline Mist (Hazard)**

*Psychic/Environmental Hazard, Lawful Neutral*

AC: —  
HP: —  
Trigger: Party encounters a dense mist threaded with crystal dust.  

Resolution Options:  
- Endurance: Con Save (DC 15) each round to resist 1 tick of Resonance.  
- Investigation (DC 15): Find vents releasing mist.  
- Stealth (DC 14): Sneak past vents without disturbing them.  
- Arcana (DC 15): Channel power crystals to temporarily shut down a vent.  
- Attack: Destroy vent (AC 14, HP 15) → spawns a passive Crystal Golem that activates if attacked.  

Success Outcome:  
- Party passes through with minimal exposure.  
- If they disable vents, gain clue about resonance “feeding.”  

Failure Outcome:  
- Each PC rolls 1d2 ticks of Resonance.  
- A golem activates if vents destroyed carelessly.
