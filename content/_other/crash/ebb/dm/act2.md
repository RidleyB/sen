The trail left by the previous team leads deeper into Plibt. As the players follow, they encounter three remnants of their progress — each camp reflecting their gradual descent into assimilation. These stops provide both atmosphere and tangible clues about what happened, while giving players growing dread before the Crown site.

## Camp Alpha – Wonder

**Read Aloud:**
*You crest a low ridge of glistening ooze to find a small encampment- some of its guild standard long-torches still smoking, but none remain lit. A few have been engulfed by a shifting slime dune.*

*Crates and satchels are neatly stacked, though half-dissolved by the ground beneath them. Notes and sketches are scattered across a central table, depicting shifting landscapes and smiling Plasmoid figures.*

This camp represents the first team's early optimism. If searched, the players find:

* **Sybil Echo Stone (DC 10 Investigation):** *Preliminary survey of crystalline structures suggests periodic resonance. Vibrational frequency appears synchronized with ambient ooze— approximately one pulse every thirty-seven heartbeats. Recommend extended observation to confirm cyclical alignment.*
* **Sybil Echo Stone (DC 15 Investigation):** *Protective ward remains intact, though strain is measurable. Psychic impressions register as low-grade, sustained pressure — not dissimilar to deep-water compression on living tissue. Interpretation remains contested: intrusion versus natural environmental phenomenon.*
* **DC 15 Investigation:** *Searching the ruined crates, you uncover a small cache of Guild power crystals (3–4 shards). These thumb-sized cyan stones are inert until slotted into something that can use them as fuel. To the trained eye, they are rare but not unusual: standard issue for discovery expeditions, meant to power arcane wards, signal flares, or the emergency recall relay.*
* **Guild Supply Crate:**
* **Ward Circle:** *The camp’s central feature is a wide circle of runes, drawn into the hardened obsidian floor and reinforced with chalk and metal rods. At first glance it looks mundane — just lines and geometric shapes — its purpose currently eludes you. The chalk is smudged, and the center of the circle seems like it is missing something.*
	* **Arcana DC 13:** *The circle is a psychic ward, designed to shield against invasive telepathic contact. Without a power source, it lies dormant. You figure with some supplies you could gather around the camp, you could recreate this ward elsewhere.*
	* **Arcana DC 18:** *The ward shows evidence of strain. Whatever pressed against it was not gentle curiosity, but a persistent, probing pressure. The failure seems less like sabotage, more like overwhelming force.*

## Skill Challenge: Sinkholes

**Read Aloud:**<br>
*The trail ahead leads across shallow pools of slick, black ooze. At first, the surface seems stable, but then—without warning—the ground ripples like disturbed water. With a wet collapse, one pool caves inward, exposing jagged crystal beneath. The air fills with a sharp tang of iron and ozone as the ooze hisses against the stone, releasing vapor like smoke from a smothered fire. More pools nearby begin to churn, threatening to give way.*

**DC:** 14, standard for targeted skills, disadvantage for untargeted.<br>
**Complexity:** 3/2 per character (successes/failures)
>Award advantage for creative skill uses. **Creative**, not a stretch.
{: .notice--info}

### Targeted Skills

- **Survival / Nature:** Predict which ooze pools are unstable by observing subtle ripples, color shifts, or bubbling.

- **Acrobatics**: Leap across narrow stable ridges of hardened ooze.

- **Athletics**: Haul a teammate out when they sink or collapse part of a path into a safer slope.

- **Perception**: Notice faint glimmers of crystal beneath the ooze, warning of a thin surface above caverns.

- **Arcana / Religion**: Recognize that the ooze is “rejecting” areas where crystal is growing upward from the Crown’s expansion, causing the rapid shifts.

### Complications (Failures)

- A character slips into a sinkhole: restrained, sinking 10 ft. into a crystalline cavern below. Another must act quickly to save them (Athletics or Rope usage).
- Shards of crystal spike upward as the caverns expand, dealing 2d6 piercing damage to anyone in range.
- Lingering vapors from the ooze impose Disadvantage on the next check (burning eyes/throat).

### Success Outcome

- The party crosses safely, but with the eerie awareness that Plibt itself is restructuring beneath them.
- They gain a narrative clue: crystalline growth is pushing upward into ooze pockets, forcing the world into violent flux.

### Failure Outcome

- One or more characters fall briefly into the crystalline caverns below, suffering damage and emerging coated in faint crystalline residue (first tick toward Unhappening).
- The path forward is destabilized — forcing the party to detour, losing time before exposure sets in.

*You look down at your arms, and see a super-fine coating of crystal dust clinging to your skin. You try to brush it off, but instead you feel is press into your skin, and disappear entirely. You feel them sink in, leaving faint lines of light that pulse once before fading. The echo lingers in your chest, like your heartbeat no longer belongs to you alone.*


## Camp Beta – Violence

**Read Aloud:**
*Where the first Camp felt ordered and deliberate, this one feels more temporary — a rushed relocation, as though the first site could no longer be trusted. Canvas shelters lean against jagged obsidian plates, crates half-unpacked, and their belongings half-scattered here and there. It’s clear the expedition hadn’t been here long before the left.**

*The ground around this new site is fractured: obsidian shards thrust upward in uneven patterns, some breaking through tents and supply crates*

*A closer look reveals they aren’t just jutting — they’re growing, crystalline veins pushing up from beneath the ooze. Several shards look displaced or broken, some almost as if they were ripped free mid-struggle.**

*A heavy stillness hangs in the air, broken only by the faint glitter of slime coating the obsidian.*

Findings include:

* **Splintered Crystals:** Obsidian-like fragments, humming faintly. Handling them causes unhappening in the crown direction.
* **Sybil Echo Stone:** *Documented evidence now supports a dual-phase cycle: fluidic ooze formation giving way to crystalline structuring, then collapsing back into fluid state. Transitional markers appear to occur everywhere with no discernable end in timeline; however, current rates of shift suggest external acceleration. Hypothesis: our presence may be a variable.*

This is the first clear evidence of the crystalline influence.

### Encounter: Glittering Oozes

When the party explores the camp, they find traces of slime that sparkle with crystalline dust. The residue quivers unnaturally. Moments later, several Glittering Oozes erupt from nearby fissures — once native ooze-forms, now warped by crystalline intrusion.

**Appearance:** Translucent sludge threaded with glowing crystal veins, faint ringing hums when they move.

**Behavior:** They thrash violently, more aggressive than natural oozes. Occasionally, they slam themselves into obsidian shards, as if drawn to resonance.

Tactics:

Lunge toward the loudest or heaviest PCs.
Explosions of crystalline dust on death (Con save vs. Resonance tick).
Immune to psychic damage (mind already fractured).

## Camp Gamma – Wrong
*Play "Where Forbidden Spirits Call", by Christopher Young*
{: .notice--info}

**Read Aloud:**
*The trail leads your party to the foot of a colossal obsidian structure, its walls sheer and unyielding, vanishing into mist above. The landscape here is warped — where ooze once flowed freely, it now hardens in places into jagged crystalline veins, like black glass forced up from the earth.*

*The remains of this camp are scattered along the base of the structure. Though nearly any of the team's belongings are in a salvageable state. It appears as though it abandoned in panic.*

**Insight 13:** *This camp feels different than the others: not simply overwhelmed by environment, but broken from within.*

**If the players investigate:**

* **Equipment:** Every item is positioned in spiral patterns. None appear used.

*At the far edge of camp, the party spots what appears to be a surviving guild-member. They stir kind of weakly at your presence, forming something of a wave before their arm slumps down.*

"Join...my...team."

*Before aid can be given, the survivor's visage begins to droop. Before your eyes you watch them begin to melt as you realize a plasmoid lays before you. It collapses into a puddle of slime — writhing, bubbling, then dispersing into inert ooze, and then crystalizing when they come in contact with the obsidian crystal.*

## Encounter – Transformed Guild Members

Soon after leaving Camp Gamma, the party is confronted by crystalline hybrids.

**Read Aloud:**
*From the mist ahead come heavy, scraping footsteps. Shapes emerge — twisted, half-humanoid, their bodies sheathed in jagged black crystal. Their faces are familiar. Guild badges glint beneath the corruption, fused into their flesh. The hybrids stagger forward, seemingly mindless, yet ready to strike.*

This combat encounter should feel tragic. PCs with ties to the Guild may recognize names. Researcher PCs may identify the condition as “Unhappening with crystal interference.”

Suggested stat blocks: *Wight* or *Ghast* with crystalline resistance (resist nonmagical bludgeoning/piercing/slashing).
