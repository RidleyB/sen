## Arrival on Plibt

**Read Aloud:**<br>
*The Sennite waygate flares to life before the six of you; its arcane lattice humming with energy. As per travel regulations you head into the portal one by one led by your Captain. You feel yourself lurch forward and begin to fall, followed by a rush of light and crushing pressure. Your stomachs heave as the magic hurls you across the cosmos. If this is your first jump, the sensation is unlike anything you’ve ever endured.*

*For a moment before the onslaught ceases, you hear nothing. Silence, vast and absolute. Then the tunnel of lights swells too bright to bear. You shut your eyes, and your boots strike something soft. Just like you’ve trained, you walk forward — and step out into another world.*

*The portal spits you into a shallow basin of black, glistening ooze. It clings to your boots with a tacky pull, stretching into threads that snap reluctantly as you move. The air is thick, damp, tinged with copper and stone; each breath tastes faintly wrong. A low hum vibrates through the ground beneath your feet, as though the world itself were resonating. Fifty feet ahead, you see remnants of a Guild camp — torn canvas, broken crates, and sigils half-sunk into the strange ground, left behind by the first team.*

### Brief Encounter with a Plasmoid
*Play "Is Someone There?, by Tor Lundvall"*

*Behind you, something stirs. A medium-sized humanoid ooze stands about fifteen feet away. Its translucent body refracts faint light into a rainbow shimmer. It tilts its head at you in a quizzical fashion, then a low hum ripples through the basin. The creature resonates with the sound — and bolts away, leaving ripples where its legs once touched.*

### Clues at the Landing Site

The first camp lies half-consumed by ooze, its supplies scattered and useless. The only intact object is a Guild-standard beacon, its crystal cracked and dark.

**Read Aloud:**<br>
*Around the camp, you see a broken supply crate half-swallowed by a dark slime, a torn Guild banner plastered flat against the ooze, and faint drag marks leading deeper into Plibt’s shifting wilds. Around, crystalline growths jut like black spires from beneath the ooze. The sharp edges catch what little light seeps through the haze, refracting it into fleeting rainbow shards that vanish as quickly as they appear. It’s unsettlingly quiet — until you realize the silence isn’t empty at all, but filled with the faint sound of gurgling… from nowhere, and everywhere at once.*

**If the players search, they can uncover:**

- **Transcript:** #1*
- **Discarded Gear** 
- **Strange Footprints:** Investigation DC 14 reveals several sets of humanoid tracks… but one trail shows a stride too wide and irregular to belong to a human.

## The First Sign of the Unhappening

As the players set out from the crash site, they encounter the first evidence of assimilation.

**Read Aloud:**
*You hear a dragging sound ahead. A Guild cloak lies half-sunken in the translucent ground. A figure is still inside it, their face slack, arms stretched unnaturally thin as if their flesh is melting into the earth. Their lips tremble: “It’s safe here. You'll see...” Then, with a sigh of relief, the body collapses, dispersing into the living ground.*

This is the first true signal of assimilation. Keep it brief. Make them feel like safety itself is suspect.

### Trail of the first team

The trail is pieced together through broken markers, gear scraps, and faint impressions in the ooze. It gives the sense of retracing steps, but warped.

Encounters in this section should emphasize **atmosphere and unease** rather than combat. Random hazards include:

* Pools of ooze that open into sudden sinkholes.
* Reflections in the mist showing the party’s faces, but subtly altered.
* A whisper that calls a PC by name.

## Skill Challenge

### **Area 1 – The Fork**

*The trail fractures into two paths, each swallowed by creeping ooze. To the north, the way glimmers faintly, as if something lurks beneath the surface. To the east, the ground is darker and irregular, trembling faintly as though waiting to collapse.*

* **Choice:** Players decide to go **north (1a)** or **east (1b)**.

### **1a – Sticky Chamber (North)**

*The air grows thick and oppressive, a syrupy humidity clinging to your skin. You notice faint glimmers in the translucent walls around you — bones, gear, and… faces. Then the ground beneath you shifts with a sickly slurp, and a massive, semi-transparent cube surges forward.*

* **Encounter:** **Gelatinous Cube**

  * The terrain is slick; failed Dex saves may restrain characters in sticky ooze patches.
  * Reward: After defeating the Cube, players can free a half-dissolved pack containing a **Potion of Greater Healing** or a **Scroll of Fireball** (high Investigation DC).
* **Exits:** Connects to **Area 2** and **Area 3**.


### **1b – The Weeping Path (East)**

*This route feels unstable from the start: the ground sloshes underfoot, every step threatening to give way. Strange humming echoes faintly from below, as though the world is sighing in protest.*

* **Mechanics:** Each character must make a **Dexterity saving throw (DC 13)** or sink waist-deep.

  * Failure: take **2d6 acid damage** and gain **1 Unhappening tick**.
  * Success: Press on, but the party emerges weary.
* **Exits:** Connects to **Area 3**.


### **Area 2 – Lost Remains**

*Half-sunken in the translucent ground lies the broken frame of a Guild explorer, their badge fused into warped gear. What little remains seems to have been preserved by the ooze.*

* **Reward:** High DC Investigation (15–17) uncovers one of the following (choose from list):
* **Dead End.**

### **Area 3 – The Ridge**

*The ground narrows into a slick ridge of hardened ooze, forcing you to edge along carefully. Below, a yawning sinkhole seethes with bubbling acid and jagged crystal teeth, opening and closing as though breathing.*

* **Choice:** Players must **Sidle along the ridge** (Dex or Acrobatics DC 14) or **risk the sinkhole**.
* **Failing the Ridge Check:** Roll 1d4 consequence:

  1. Lose a pack item (falls into the acid).
  2. Take **2d10 acid damage**.
  3. Gain **1 Unhappening tick** (contact with crystallized vapor).
  4. Get knocked prone, forcing another ally to help.
* **Exit:** Leads to **Area 4**.

### **Area 4 – Safe Junction**

*For the first time in what feels like hours, the ground stabilizes beneath you. No shifting, no bubbling, no distant hums. Just silence.*

* **Guild Marker:** With a Perception DC 13, characters spot a **Guild sigil etched into hardened ooze** — a subtle mark left by the previous team. **Effect:** Any character who finds the mark regains **1 Inspiration**.

### **Area 5 – Ooze Pools (Final Hazard)**

Like the ridge, but needing two successess before two failures. Be creative.

## A Likeness

**Read Aloud:**<br>
*Across the mire, a figure rises from the ooze. At first it’s just a shimmer in the haze — but as it steps forward, you recognize the face. Your face. Not perfect: its mouth opens too wide, its eyes hold no light, but the stance, the posture, the gaze — it’s unmistakably you.*

Anyone with a perception of 14 can see it.

* If the PC attempts to **investigate, approach, or interact**, have them make a **Wisdom saving throw (DC 14)**.
* **On a failure:** They gain **1 tick of Unhappening**, as the Ebb tugs on their sense of self — their outline softening, their reflection feeling more *real* than their own body.
* **On a success:** The double dissolves into the pool like a ripple swallowed by silence, but the PC feels its echo inside their skull: *“Closer.”*
* If multiple PCs see the double, they may each perceive it as their own likeness simultaneously, further unsettling the group.

## A Whisper

**Read Aloud:**<br>
*As you steady yourself, you hear it — soft, impossibly close, right at your ear.*
*“{Character’s Name}…”*
The sound carries warmth, like a loved one calling you awake. But when you turn, no one is there. Your companions haven’t even looked your way.

**If the PC investigates / follows the voice:**<br>
*The whisper comes again, this time ahead of you, drifting just beyond reach. it feels as though it wants to show you something.*

*Each step draws you further from the path laid out by the previous team. The world growing muffled and viscous, as if you’re moving through water. The air thickens, carrying the coppery tang of ooze, until you feel it tugging at your boots, your breath, your very thoughts.*

* If the player continues following the voice, they must make a **Wisdom saving throw (DC 16)**.
* On a **success**, they resist — but the voice lingers in the back of their mind, promising it will return.
* On a **failure**, they gain **1 tick of Unhappening**, as the Ebb pulls at their form.

**Read Aloud:**<br>
*You look down reflexively as the ground tugs at your boots, and you see that your feet have become covered in ooze. You watch it begin to climb up your leather boots, and begins to transform your boots into the very same ooze. Rapidly your body begins to change; you feel the warn, wet slime begin to replace your skin, and your body feels heavy, that same gravity pulling you down towards the earth and then-*

Clap loudly once.

*You snap out of it- your body **seems** normal. But...how long were you standing here? You look down at your slime-covered boots...and it doesn't begin to climb like it did before.*
