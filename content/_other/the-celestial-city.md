The Celestial City; a sprawling magical metropolis renowned for its adaptive architecture and rich history. The city is built in concentric rings, each serving distinct purposes and housing different factions, guilds, and important structures. Its enchanted walls can expand to accommodate growth, creating a dynamic urban landscape. The outer rings host dense residential neighborhoods, while the inner rings feature older, historically significant buildings.

Sen has no countryside—no hills, mountains, or plains. It exists as a single, sprawling city at the center of the universe. The weather is temperate, with mild rains providing occasional cleansing. Fluffy clouds drift across a crystal-blue sky as the sun, **Solara**, traces its path. Several moons orbit Sen, and stars fill the night sky as they would in any world.

## Core

{{ image1 }}

At Sen’s heart stands the **Grand Cathedral of the Church of the Union**, a monument to the city’s divine patron, **Anzu**, and the ancient pantheon of Qon. The cathedral serves as a place of worship, spiritual guidance, and community gathering, its towering spires visible from nearly every part of the city. The **Lord’s Hands**, devouts of the church, welcome all who seek guidance or aid.

## The Inner Rings

Encircling the Grand Cathedral, the inner rings host Sen’s most prominent public buildings: guild headquarters, judiciary halls, and prestigious colleges. This area is a hub for scholars, officials, and influential figures, fostering governance, knowledge, and civic engagement.

## The Outer Rings

Beyond the inner rings lie the sprawling outer rings, divided into diverse boroughs with distinct character and culture. Markets, residential areas, and commercial districts intertwine to form a vibrant urban mosaic. The city’s magical walls continually expand to accommodate growth and newcomers, allowing Sen to integrate new cultures and innovations seamlessly.

## Beyond the Wall

Peering over Sen’s towering walls reveals the vast cosmos below. The city’s construction remains a marvel of ancient magic, confounding scientists and mages alike. Many believe Sen floats amidst the stars, a city suspended in the universe itself.
